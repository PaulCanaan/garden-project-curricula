# The Garden Project Curricula

Biblical, Christ-centered, formation-oriented curricula designed to help people understand the story of Scripture, encounter Jesus, and grow into mature disciples.

This repository contains Bible study curricula, leader guides, workshops, frameworks, teaching resources, and supporting materials developed through The Garden Project.

## Our Curriculum Philosophy

The Garden Project curricula are shaped by three design principles:

### 1. Biblically Outlined

Each curriculum follows the structure, themes, movements, and theological logic of Scripture rather than imposing an external system onto the biblical text.

### 2. Christ-Centered

Each curriculum seeks to show how the whole biblical story finds its fulfillment, unity, and climax in Jesus Christ.

### 3. Formation-Oriented

The goal is not information alone.

Each curriculum is designed to shape how participants:

* See God
* Read Scripture
* Understand themselves
* Live in Christian community
* Follow Jesus
* Participate in God’s mission

## The Curriculum Journey

The curricula are designed as a progressive learning journey, moving from foundational biblical literacy toward deeper biblical theology and mature theological reflection.

```text
Planted.
    ↓
Covenant, Kingdom, and People of God
    ↓
God With Us
    ↓
Thus Says the LORD
```

The sequence moves from broad foundations to more focused biblical-theological study.

Participants may enter at different points depending on their biblical knowledge, spiritual maturity, and formation needs.

## Current Curricula

### Planted.

A foundational Bible study introducing the storyline of Scripture, the gospel, Christian identity, spiritual formation, and the life of discipleship.

Core movement:

> Seed → Sapling → Tree → Forest → Plant Again

Primary audience:

* New believers
* Spiritually curious participants
* Christians seeking biblical foundations
* Small groups and discipleship cohorts

### Covenant, Kingdom, and People of God

A biblical theology study tracing God’s covenant purposes through Scripture and exploring how God forms a people under his reign.

Major themes include:

* Creation and humanity
* Noah
* Abraham
* Israel and Sinai
* David
* The New Covenant
* Jesus and the people of God

Primary audience:

* Growing disciples
* Small groups
* Bible study cohorts
* Emerging leaders

### God With Us

A temple theology curriculum tracing the theme of God’s presence from creation to new creation.

Core movement:

> Creation → Eden → Tabernacle → Temple → Christ → Church → New Creation

Major themes include:

* God’s presence
* Sacred space
* Priesthood
* Holiness
* Exile and return
* Jesus as the true temple
* The church as God’s dwelling place
* New creation

Primary audience:

* Intermediate Bible students
* Ministry leaders
* Biblical theology cohorts
* Churches seeking a whole-Bible framework

### Thus Says the LORD

A curriculum on the Prophets, prophetic theology, and the formation of God’s people through the Word of the Lord.

Major themes include:

* Exodus
* Covenant
* Temple
* Kingship
* The people of God
* Judgment and hope
* New creation
* Christ as the fulfillment of the prophetic witness

Core movement:

> Learn to Hear → Walk with the Prophets → Hear the One Voice → Embody the Voice

Primary audience:

* Advanced Bible students
* Teachers and leaders
* Biblical theology cohorts
* Participants preparing to teach Scripture

## How to Use These Materials

These resources may be used in:

* Churches
* Small groups
* Classes
* Discipleship cohorts
* Leadership development
* Workshops
* Retreats
* Personal study
* Ministry training

Before teaching a curriculum:

1. Read the curriculum overview.
2. Review the leader guide.
3. Adapt examples to your local context.
4. Preserve the biblical and theological structure.
5. Avoid removing formation practices merely to shorten the material.
6. Prepare facilitators before launching a group.
7. Gather participant feedback after completion.
