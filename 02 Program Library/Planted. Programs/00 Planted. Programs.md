## Facilitation commitments

Every program should preserve these norms:

- Participation is invited, never coerced.
- Explain physical, emotional, food, and accessibility factors before beginning.
- Offer an equivalent low-risk or observational role without requiring disclosure.
- Do not use surprise, shame, pain, forced exertion, forced eating, or trauma disclosure as teaching devices.
- Keep the theological claim proportionate to the activity; an illustration is not proof.
- Debrief long enough for participants to interpret their own experience before the facilitator explains it.
- Protect confidentiality while clearly naming the limits of confidentiality and pastoral escalation.
- Follow the church's safeguarding rules and emergency procedures.

## Standard flow

1. **Frame:** state the purpose, boundaries, opt-out, and safety instructions.
2. **Experience:** run the simplest form of the activity.
3. **Notice:** ask what participants felt, saw, and did without correcting them.
4. **Interpret:** connect patterns to Scripture and the governing framework.
5. **Integrate:** name a relationship, practice, prayer, or next step.
6. **Review:** record what worked, what caused friction, and what should change.
