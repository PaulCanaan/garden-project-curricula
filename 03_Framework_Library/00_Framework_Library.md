# Framework Library

Reusable models for understanding Scripture, guiding formation, making decisions, and designing teaching. A framework is a tool, not the truth itself: use it when it clarifies reality, and set it aside when it begins to flatten mystery or replace wise judgment.

Interpretive map: [[Frameworks MOC]]

This library is the internal source for frameworks used across [[00 Bible Study Curricula|curricula]], [[00 Program Library|programs]], [[00 Workshop Library|workshops]], and [[00 CoJourner Program|CoJourner formation]]. Public teaching editions are eventually stewarded by [[00 The Garden Project Ministry|The Garden Project]].

## Biblical story and theological integration

- [[Already Not Yet]] — hold together the inaugurated and future dimensions of God's kingdom
- [[Exodus Road]] — salvation as the way out, the way through, and the way in
- [[Garden Framework]] — receive, cultivate, bear fruit, and multiply within God's world
- [[Seed-Form Typology]] — trace historical realities that grow toward fuller realization in Christ and new creation
- [[Five Echoes]] — listen upward, backward, inward, outward, and forward within prophetic literature
- [[T2T]] — move from tabernacle to temple and from temple identity to tabernacling mission

## Formation and discipleship

- [[Belonging Beholding Becoming]] — formation through covenant identity, attention to Christ, and Spirit-enabled transformation
- [[Faith Muscle]] — train trust through small, repeated acts of dependence and obedience
- [[07 Curricula/03 Framework Library/Know Grow Go/Know Grow Go|Know Grow Go]] — know God, grow into Christlikeness and purpose, and go in love and mission
- [[Embody the Voice]] — bear witness through discernment, compassion, truthful speech, Kingdom life, and hope

## Interpretation, discernment, and action

- [[Gospel Flow]] — move from who God is and what God has done to who we are and how we live
- [[Grace Truth Matrix]] — discern whether a response holds together truthful clarity and gracious love
- [[HABITS]] — form faithful knowers through humility, attention, bias awareness, integrity, teachability, and stewardship of truth
- [[Ontology Eschatology Matrix]] — evaluate a practice by the reality it assumes and the future it serves
- [[PLANT]] — pray, listen, articulate, name next steps, and try again
- [[SOAP]] — read Scripture through Scripture, observation, application, and prayer

## Choosing a framework

- **Reading a passage:** [[SOAP]], [[Seed-Form Typology]], or [[Gospel Flow]]; use [[Five Echoes]] when reading prophetic literature
- **Explaining salvation:** [[Exodus Road]], [[Already Not Yet]], or [[Gospel Flow]]
- **Tracing temple and mission:** [[T2T]], supported by [[Seed-Form Typology]] and [[Already Not Yet]]
- **Designing formation:** [[Belonging Beholding Becoming]], [[Faith Muscle]], [[Garden Framework]], or [[Embody the Voice]] for Christian witness
- **Practicing faithful knowing:** [[HABITS]], [[Grace Truth Matrix]], or [[Ontology Eschatology Matrix]]
- **Planning discipleship:** [[07 Curricula/03 Framework Library/Know Grow Go/Know Grow Go|Know Grow Go]], [[Garden Framework]], or [[PLANT]]
- **Making a pastoral or ethical decision:** [[Grace Truth Matrix]] or [[Ontology Eschatology Matrix]]
- **Designing a curriculum or program:** combine one story framework, one formation framework, and one action framework

## Maturity guide

- **Developing:** coherent enough to test, but language or boundaries still need refinement
- **Developed:** source-grounded, documented, and ready for repeated use
- **Validated:** used in multiple settings with recorded feedback and revision

## Library maintenance

When revising a framework:

1. Preserve its core problem and insight.
2. Name its biblical and theological grounding.
3. Include a worked example.
4. State limits and likely misuses.
5. Record evidence from real use before raising its maturity.

## Framework-to-program relationships

Some conceptual tools become more effective when taught through embodied experience. When that happens, preserve the theological content and provenance but classify the reusable artifact by its primary pedagogy.

- [[Grace Truth Matrix]] → [[The Grid]]
- Foundation → Frame → Dwelling → [[Building A House]]

*Building A House* now lives in the Program Library because its primary function is an experiential entrance into *God With Us*, not a standalone conceptual framework.

## Ministry stewardship

A framework becomes a public resource only when its claims, limits, examples, attribution, and likely misuses are documented. Publication status follows [[03 Publishing and Stewardship Pipeline]] rather than maturity alone.
