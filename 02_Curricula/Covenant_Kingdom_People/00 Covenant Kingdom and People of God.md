# Covenant, Kingdom, and People of God

A five-week Bible study tracing the unity and diversity of the biblical covenants, God's pledge as the ground of covenant reality, and the formation of a people who live under God's reign.

## Transformation promise

Participants will learn to read Scripture as one covenantal story centered on Jesus Christ and to inhabit that story through worship, identity, community, obedience, and hope.

## Governing thesis

God's covenant faithfulness moves creation toward communion: God establishes his kingdom by dwelling with a people who know him, bear his image, mediate his blessing, and participate in the renewal of creation.

## Course arc

1. [[01 Week 1 - Universal Covenant|Universal Covenant]] — creation, fall, flood, and God's commitment to preserve and restore.
2. [[02 Week 2 - Ancestral Covenant|Ancestral Covenant]] — Abraham, sojourning, land, offspring, and blessing to the nations.
3. [[03 Week 3 - National Covenant|National Covenant]] — exodus, Sinai, priestly identity, presence, and formation in the wilderness.
4. [[04 Week 4 - Messianic Covenant|Messianic Covenant]] — David's house, kingdom, exile, prophetic hope, and typology.
5. [[05 Week 5 - New Covenant|New Covenant]] — Jesus as the new King who brings the new exodus and inaugurates new creation.

## Recurring learning rhythm

Each session moves through five modes:

1. **Narrative** — retell a major movement in the biblical story.
2. **Discussion** — surface interpretive questions and tensions.
3. **Discovery** — compare the elements of a particular covenant.
4. **Application** — reflect on identity, faithfulness, and covenant life.
5. **Worship and prayer** — respond to God rather than stopping at information.

## Covenant discovery lens

Every covenant is examined through:

- Context of covenant-making
- Mediator and covenant parties
- Scope
- Agreements, responsibilities, or conditions
- Covenant sign
- God's pledged blessing
- Consequences or curse
- Fulfillment in Christ
- Distinctive contribution to the biblical story

See [[A Covenant Comparison|Covenant Comparison]].

## Major integrative movements

### Chaos → cosmos → new creation

The biblical story repeatedly moves from disorder to God-given order, through human rebellion into renewed chaos, and toward restoration through God's covenant faithfulness.

### Exile → exodus → presence

Exile is estrangement from God's presence; exodus is God's act of bringing a people near. Deliverance is for worship, communion, and vocation.

### Promise → kingdom → Messiah

The Abrahamic promises of offspring, land, and blessing take royal form in the Davidic covenant and reach their fulfillment in Jesus, the everlasting King.

### Rescue → formation → inheritance

The [[Exodus Road]] names the pattern: the way out of bondage, the way through formative wilderness, and the way into promised life with God.

### Covenant → people → mission

Covenant creates a people whose identity and shared life display God's character and mediate blessing to the nations.
