# Week 5 — Expansion

## New Jerusalem — The Garden-City Temple

**Governing question:** How does God's dwelling expand until creation becomes holy space?

## Draft word-study focus

- *skēnē* — tent/tabernacle/dwelling; do not gloss the noun itself as the act “tabernacling”
- *naos* — sanctuary/temple; attend to its referent in each New Testament context

## Key texts

- Isaiah 65–66
- Ezekiel 47
- Revelation 21–22

## Discovery

- Why does John see no temple building in the New Jerusalem?
- How do garden, city, river, tree, throne, priesthood, and nations converge?
- What images suggest that sacred space has become creation-wide?
- How is the Lamb both temple and light?

## Teaching movement

The Bible begins with ordered creation and a garden dwelling and ends with a garden-city filled by God's presence. The New Jerusalem has no separate sanctuary because God Almighty and the Lamb are its temple. The goal is not escape from creation but God dwelling with redeemed humanity in renewed creation.

## Formation

- **Reflect:** What fears and hopes surface when imagining God fully dwelling with humanity?
- **Practice:** Treat creation, neighbor, work, and worship in light of their promised renewal.
- **Prayer:** Ask for endurance and holy imagination shaped by God's coming home.

## Exit

Transition into [[T2T]]: a people who are the Spirit's temple practice tabernacling presence through worship, witness, and hospitality during their earthly sojourn.
