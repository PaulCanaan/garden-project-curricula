# Prophetic World Timeline and Context Guide

This is the recurring orientation map for [[00 Thus Says The LORD]]. Dates are approximate and should be reviewed against the selected scholarly sources before publication.

| Era | Political world | Prophetic witnesses emphasized | Interpretive questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| United and divided kingdoms | Israel and Judah; royal and covenant crises | prophetic background in Former Prophets | Which king and kingdom? What covenant responsibility? |
| Assyrian crisis | Assyria dominates the region; northern kingdom falls | Isaiah; Hosea; Amos; Micah; Jonah; Nahum and others | What alliances, idols, injustices, and false trusts are addressed? |
| Babylonian crisis | Judah's final kings; Jerusalem and temple fall | Jeremiah; Habakkuk | Why is judgment coming, and what hope remains? |
| Exile | Judeans live under Babylon; Persia later rises | Ezekiel; Daniel | How are presence, identity, worship, and faithfulness sustained away from home? |
| Persian restoration | Return, rebuilding, and disappointed expectations | Haggai; Zechariah; Malachi | Why does return from exile not equal final restoration? |

## Five-context worksheet

For every primary passage, record:

1. **Historical-cultural:** speaker, audience, ruler, empire, location, crisis, covenant situation
2. **Literary:** genre, unit boundaries, book placement, repeated images, rhetorical movement
3. **Grammatical:** speakers, parallel lines, key terms, metaphors, contrasts, discourse markers
4. **Theological:** what is revealed about YHWH, sin, judgment, salvation, people, nations, and hope?
5. **Canonical:** what earlier Scripture is recalled, and how does the trajectory develop toward Christ and new creation?

## Guardrail

Context is not a preliminary obstacle to “the real message.” It is the world in which God's address becomes intelligible. Application should grow from this world rather than leap over it.
