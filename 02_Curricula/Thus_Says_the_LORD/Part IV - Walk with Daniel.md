# Part IV — Walk with Daniel

## God Reigns — The New King

**Big question:** Who truly rules history?

## Chapter 1 — Faithful in Babylon

- **World:** life under Babylonian and Persian imperial power
- **Texts:** Daniel 1–6
- **Form:** court narratives whose faithful practices prepare readers for the visions
- **Motifs:** wisdom, worship, resistance to idolatry, public witness, divine sovereignty

## Chapter 2 — The Kingdom of the Son of Man

- **Texts:** Daniel 2; 7
- **Forms:** symbolic dream and apocalyptic vision
- **Motifs:** beastly empire, the Ancient of Days, one like a son of man, saints, dominion, judgment
- **Canonical trajectory:** Jesus' Son of Man identity and testimony in Matthew 26

## Chapter 3 — The Kingdom That Cannot Be Shaken

- **Texts:** Daniel 9; 12
- **Motifs:** confession, waiting, suffering, judgment, resurrection, everlasting life
- **Canonical horizon:** Christ's vindication and Revelation's critique of beastly empire

## Formation movement

Practice faithful presence without assimilation, discern the beastly character of power, pray in solidarity with God's people, and endure under the reign of the Son of Man.
