# Thus Says The LORD

## Hearing the Voice of God Through the Prophets

> **The prophets received the Word of the LORD. Jesus is the Word made flesh. The Church bears faithful witness to the Word.**

*Thus Says the LORD* is the level-400 Garden Project curriculum on prophetic literature. Participants learn to hear the prophets in their historical and literary worlds, walk with their diverse witnesses through judgment and salvation, recognize their convergence in Christ, and embody faithful witness as the Church.

## Curriculum identity

- **Governing biblical theme:** God's restorative work announced through the prophets
- **Governing metaphor:** hearing one divine Voice through many faithful echoes
- **Controlling question:** How does God restore his Kingdom after the failure and exile of his people?
- **Emotional center:** holy confrontation, lament, courage, and resilient hope
- **Theological foundation:** biblical theology of prophecy, covenant, judgment and salvation, Christological fulfillment, and ecclesial witness
- **Signature assets:** [[Five Echoes]], [[Embody the Voice]], prophetic context maps, and the movement from many prophetic voices to the one Word fulfilled in Christ

## Formation promise

Participants will grow as readers who can locate a prophetic text in its historical, cultural, literary, and grammatical context; trace its covenantal message of judgment and salvation; follow its canonical trajectory toward Christ; and bear truthful, compassionate, courageous, embodied, and hopeful witness.

## The formation journey

| Stage | Movement | Invitation | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entrance | **Learn to Hear** | Enter the prophets' world and receive a listening guide | Read prophetic literature faithfully |
| Journey | **Walk with the Prophets** | Accompany Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Daniel, and the Twelve | Recognize the diversity and unity of prophetic witness |
| Exit | **Embody the Voice** | Follow the prophets to Christ and the Spirit-formed Church | Bear faithful witness to the Word |

This is the prophets curriculum's expression of the Garden Project's movement–institution dynamic: participants do not merely study the prophetic institution; they walk with the prophets until they can inhabit its witness under Christ.

## Three design principles

### Biblically outlined

The middle journey respects the literary identity and historical world of the prophetic books. The major sections are organized by each corpus's principal biblical-theological contribution, while the Twelve use a historical before–during–after exile survey.

### Christ-centered

Christ is not attached as a final devotional thought. The canonical trajectories of new exodus, new covenant, new temple, new King, and new people converge in the incarnate Word, who teaches the Church how to read the prophets in [[Part VI - From One Voice to One Witness]].

### Formation-oriented

The course begins by forming listeners and ends by forming witnesses. [[Five Echoes]] serves interpretation; [[Embody the Voice]] turns prophetic theology toward discernment, compassion, truthful speech, Kingdom life, and hope.

## Exegetical rhythm

Each prophetic journey should consistently integrate rather than isolate:

1. **Historical and cultural context** — prophet, audience, king, empire, crisis, covenant situation
2. **Literary and grammatical context** — book design, genre, oracle, poetry, parallelism, metaphor, vision, sign act
3. **Theological message** — YHWH's character, covenant, judgment, salvation, remnant, Kingdom, presence, and hope
4. **Canonical and Christological trajectory** — responsible development and New Testament use
5. **Formation and witness** — worship, repentance, communal faithfulness, mission, courage, and hope

[[Five Echoes]] is used as a recurring sidebar and reflection prompt, not as a substitute for these contexts or as a purported scholarly consensus.

## Modular resolutions

| Resolution | Suggested shape | What must remain |
|---|---|---|
| Intensive | Entrance, five theological movements, and exit in one retreat | Original context, judgment–salvation rhythm, Christological culmination, faithful witness |
| Core | Seven sessions: one per part | All three chapters treated as movements within each session |
| Extended | Twenty-one sessions: one per chapter | Full context, text study, Five Echoes reflection, and formation practice |
| Deep study | Twenty-one sessions plus seminars and book club | Languages, maps, reception history, extended sources, and leader research |

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce prophets to predictors, contemporary political mascots, or coded end-times charts.
- Do not bypass original audiences and covenant crises when moving toward Christ.
- Hold judgment and salvation together; neither condemnation nor optimism alone represents the prophetic witness.
- The Church bears witness to the revealed Word under Christ and the apostles; it does not assume the canonical office of Isaiah or Jeremiah.
- Treat disputed dates and editorial questions honestly, especially Joel and the formation of the Twelve.
- Countercultural witness means faithfulness, not reflexive contrarianism.
