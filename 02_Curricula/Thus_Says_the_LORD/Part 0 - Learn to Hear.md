# Part 0 — Learn to Hear

## Preparing to Listen

**Goal:** Equip participants to enter the prophetic world before interpreting individual books. This entrance creates responsible listeners rather than offering a shortcut around exegesis.

## Chapter 1 — Who Were the Prophets?

**Governing question:** What did a prophet do within Israel's covenant life?

- Prophet, priest, and king as distinct but related institutions
- Former and Latter Prophets in the Tanakh
- Prophets as divine spokespersons, covenant messengers, intercessors, watchmen, critics, and heralds of hope
- True and false prophets; message, character, covenant fidelity, and communal discernment
- “Thus says the LORD” as received authority rather than originality

**Anchor texts:** Deuteronomy 13; 18:9–22; 1 Kings 18; Jeremiah 1; 23; Ezekiel 3; Amos 3:7–8.

## Chapter 2 — Reading the Prophets Well

**Governing question:** What contexts must be heard together?

| Context | Questions and content |
|---|---|
| Historical-cultural | United and divided kingdoms; Assyria, Babylon, Persia; exile and return; prophet, audience, ruler, empire, crisis |
| Literary | Poetry, prose, oracle, lawsuit, lament, vision, apocalypse, symbolic action, book-level design |
| Grammatical | Parallelism, metaphor, repetition, wordplay, shifting speakers, tense/aspect, discourse boundaries |
| Theological | Covenant blessings and curses, holiness, idolatry, judgment and salvation, Day of the LORD, remnant, restoration |
| Canonical | Place within the Tanakh and unified biblical Story; New Testament reception; fulfillment in Christ without erasing original meaning |

Use [[Prophetic World Timeline and Context Guide]] throughout the course.

## Chapter 3 — The Five Echoes

[[Five Echoes]] is a listening guide taught once and practiced repeatedly:

- **Upward:** Who is YHWH calling the people to worship?
- **Backward:** What acts and promises of God are remembered?
- **Inward:** What sins, idols, or heart conditions are exposed?
- **Outward:** How should God's people live together and before the nations?
- **Forward:** What judgment, salvation, or future hope is announced?

The Echoes generate observation and formation questions. They do not replace historical-grammatical exegesis or claim to be the prophets' universally recognized scholarly outline.

## Session outcome

Participants can explain why prophecy is more than prediction, locate a passage within the prophetic world, identify its form and covenant situation, and begin using [[Listening for the Echoes]].
