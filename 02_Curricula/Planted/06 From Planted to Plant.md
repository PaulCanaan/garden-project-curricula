# From Planted to Plant

## Purpose

Move from individual growth to the Church as a fruitful, interdependent community sent to make disciples and cultivate life for others.

## Central question

What does a planted person become, and what are we sent to cultivate together?

## Key texts

- 1 Corinthians 3:5–9 — planting, watering, and God's gift of growth
- Matthew 28:18–20 — disciples who baptize, teach, and live under Christ's presence
- John 15:1–17 — abiding, fruit, love, and lasting fruit
- Revelation 22:1–2 — tree of life, abundant fruit, and healing for the nations

## Program and frameworks

## Teaching movements

### From tree to forest

Individual growth matters, but the Church is not a collection of isolated trees. It is an interdependent ecosystem in which gifts, burdens, practices, and leadership are shared.

### Bearing fruit

Fruit is received through abiding in Christ and becomes love, character, service, witness, justice, hospitality, and healing for others.

### Sowing seeds

Mature disciples accompany others. Multiplication is not recruitment or cloning; it is participation in God's mission by equipping disciples who can multiply disciples.

### Know, grow, go

The source presents “Know God → live on purpose → go change the world” beneath the title Know / Grow / Go. The canonical framework retains the memorable three movements while interpreting the middle as growth into Christlikeness and purposeful vocation.

### PLANT

Pray → Listen → Articulate → Next Steps → Try Again. The loop keeps mission prayerful, relational, concrete, and open to learning.

## Storyline

The Bible's garden imagery reaches a city-garden whose tree bears fruit and whose leaves heal the nations. The Story moves from seed to tree to forest—and from being planted to planting again.

## Formation response

- Name the community in which your growth is being cultivated.
- Identify fruit meant for another person's good.
- Use PLANT to choose one small, accountable missional next step.
