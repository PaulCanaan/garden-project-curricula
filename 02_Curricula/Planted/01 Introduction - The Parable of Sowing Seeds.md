# Introduction — The Parable of Sowing Seeds

## Purpose

Introduce *Planted.* as the story of three interwoven seeds—the seed of Jesus, the seed of the Kingdom, and the seed of faith—and invite participants to attend to the condition of the soil in which the Kingdom message is received.

## Central question

What helps the message of the Kingdom take root and become fruitful in a human life?

## Key texts

- Matthew 13:3–9 — the parable of the sower
- Matthew 13:18–23 — Jesus' interpretation of the four soils
- John 1:14 — Jesus as the fullness of grace and truth

## Teaching movement

1. The seed is the message of the Kingdom.
2. The four soils reveal different conditions of reception, endurance, distraction, and fruitfulness.
3. Spiritual formation involves both truthful diagnosis and gracious cultivation.
4. Jesus does not merely offer a technique for better soil; he embodies the grace and truth through which life grows.

- **Program:** [[The Grid]]
- **Framework:** [[Grace Truth Matrix]]

The source manuscript introduces the four-quadrant experience before naming the framework. Preserve that experiential order: let participants notice the dynamics first, then use grace and truth to interpret them.

## Formation response

- Where does the Kingdom message currently meet hardness, shallowness, pressure, anxiety, or competing desire?
- What truthful naming is needed?
- What form of grace, patience, community, or practice could help cultivate receptivity?
