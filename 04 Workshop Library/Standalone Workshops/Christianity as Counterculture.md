# Christianity as Counterculture

A standalone formation workshop that presents the missional focus of [[00 The Garden Project Ministry|The Garden Project]]. It moves from Romans 12:1–2 and the pressures of a post-Christian culture toward inner transformation, Christian community, culture-making, and faithful witness.

## Governing question

**How can Christians become a transformed people whose shared life bears truthful and gracious witness within culture?**

## Ministry thesis

**We plant gardens by growing people.**

The workshop should preserve its relational rather than combative posture: conversation before argument, formation before performance, and culture-making as communal witness rather than reaction.

## Curriculum relationship

This workshop may serve as a practice environment for [[Embody the Voice]] within [[00 Thus Says The LORD]]. Its countercultural posture should remain prophetic in the biblical sense: faithful to Christ rather than oppositional for its own sake. The workshop can help participants practice seeing cultural stories, feeling with holy compassion, speaking truth with grace, living a credible communal alternative, and hoping in God's future.
