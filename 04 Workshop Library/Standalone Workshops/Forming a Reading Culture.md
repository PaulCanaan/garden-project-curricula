# Forming a Reading Culture

A standalone workshop for forming communities that approach texts with humility, read Scripture as a typologically unified Story centered on Christ, and become more Christlike together.

## Governing question

**How can a community learn to read in a way that helps it inhabit God's Story?**

## Intended movement

Posture before the text → Christ at the center → the Bible's unified Story → already and not yet → communal formation into Christlikeness

Reading is treated as a formative communal practice, not information accumulation. The desired outcome is a reading culture in which books and Scripture deepen attention, conversation, wisdom, and life with Jesus.
