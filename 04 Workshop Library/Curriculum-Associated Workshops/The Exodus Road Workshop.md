# The Exodus Road Workshop

A workshop accompanying [[00 Covenant Kingdom and People of God|Covenant, Kingdom, and People of God]] that invites participants to inhabit the recurring exodus movement of God's covenant people.

## Governing movement

1. **The Way Out** — deliverance from bondage and chaos by God's covenant faithfulness.
2. **The Way Through** — testing, provision, formation, worship, and renewed trust.
3. **The Way In** — communion, inheritance, abundant life, vocation, and the fruit of the Spirit.

The workshop should preserve the covenant curriculum's identity as the story of the sojourners. It does not reduce salvation to escape; deliverance leads through formation into presence, promise, and participation.

## Signature relationships

- [[Exodus Road]] — governing framework
- 🛶 Ark / deliverance and covenant promise
- 🛣️ Road / wilderness formation
- 🍷 Cup / new covenant communion and abundant life
