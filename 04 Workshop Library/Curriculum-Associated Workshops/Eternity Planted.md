# Eternity Planted

A featured workshop accompanying [[00 Planted|Planted.]] that enlarges participants' vision from personal salvation to the Kingdom, the union of heaven and earth, God's dwelling with humanity, and new creation.

## Governing question

**Where is the Story going?**

## Intended movement

Personal salvation → God's Kingdom → heaven and earth → God's presence → eternal life → new creation → participation in God's mission

The workshop expands the fourth movement of *Planted.* without functioning as an additional curriculum chapter. Its work is perspective: participants learn to see present life from the horizon of God's promised future.
