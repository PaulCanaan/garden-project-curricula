# The Church Workshop

A capstone workshop for *God With Us* in which participants discover the Church as a Spirit-filled, Christ-shaped, priestly people sent into the world.

## Governing question

**If God makes his home among and within his people, how should the Church inhabit the world?**

## Intended movement

Indwelling Spirit → Body of Christ and living sacrifices → priestly service → hospitality and shared life → witness in the world

The workshop is the formative culmination of the temple storyline, not an abstract workshop on ecclesiology. Participants move from recognizing God's dwelling places to understanding themselves as living stones within God's house.
