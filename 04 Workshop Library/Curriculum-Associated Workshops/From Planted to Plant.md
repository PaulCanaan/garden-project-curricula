# From Planted to Plant

A featured commissioning workshop accompanying [[00 Planted|Planted.]] that moves participants from personal growth toward interdependent church life, vocation, disciple-making, and mission.

## Governing question

**What does a planted person become?**

## Intended movement

Planted seed → growing tree → shared forest → abundant life → community → vocation → multiplication

The workshop completes the fifth movement of *Planted.* without becoming another week of content. It helps participants look outward and receive a next faithful responsibility within the life and mission of the Church.

## Signature relationships

- [[The Bond]] — embodied experience of centralized and interdependent community
- [[07 Curricula/03 Framework Library/Know Grow Go/Know Grow Go|Know Grow Go]] — discipleship and participation
- [[PLANT]] — evangelistic attentiveness and action
- [[00 CoJourner Program|CoJourner]] — ongoing accompaniment and multiplication
