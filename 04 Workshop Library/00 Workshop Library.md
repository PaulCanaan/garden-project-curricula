# Workshop Library

The Workshop Library holds focused formation experiences developed and eventually published through [[00 The Garden Project Ministry|The Garden Project]]. A workshop helps people practice or inhabit a dimension of the biblical Story through teaching, Scripture, reflection, programs, and communal response.

Interpretive map: [[Workshops MOC]]

## Workshops are not programs or curricula

| Form | Primary purpose | Typical scale | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Framework** | Make a recurring pattern understandable and portable | A teaching tool used in many settings | [[Grace Truth Matrix]] |
| **Program** | Create one memorable experience that unlocks one insight | 15–45 minutes inside another gathering | [[The Grid]] or [[Building A House]] |
| **Workshop** | Form understanding and practice around a focused question | 60 minutes to a day; may contain programs | [[Eternity Planted]] |
| **Curriculum** | Lead people through a sustained biblical-theological journey | Multiple sessions at adjustable depth | [[00 Planted|Planted.]] |

A program may serve as the entrance to a workshop or curriculum. A workshop may accompany a curriculum without becoming another chapter. A curriculum may have featured workshops that enlarge its vision or commission participants after the core journey.

## Shared Garden Project DNA

Every workshop should be:

1. **Biblically outlined** — its movement arises from Scripture and the biblical Story rather than a collection of topical points.
2. **Christ-centered** — its theme, interpretation, and response converge on Jesus rather than bypassing him.
3. **Formation-oriented** — it seeks a changed way of seeing, loving, practicing, relating, or witnessing rather than information alone.

The wider family also favors whole-person learning, memorable imagery, prayer, worship, reflection, community, and a meaningful next step. Unity does not require sameness: each workshop should preserve the governing metaphor, theological foundation, emotional center, and signature assets of its source curriculum or ministry purpose.

## Curriculum-associated workshops

### Planted. — life, Kingdom, and mission

- [[Eternity Planted]] — a featured vision workshop asking where the Story is going: Kingdom, heaven and earth, God's presence, eternal life, and new creation
- [[From Planted to Plant]] — a featured commissioning workshop asking what a planted person becomes: community, vocation, disciple-making, and multiplication

These workshops complete rather than extend the five-movement core. *Eternity Planted* looks upward and forward; *From Planted to Plant* looks outward and sends.

### Covenant, Kingdom, and People of God / Sojourners; — promise and pilgrimage

- [[The Exodus Road Workshop]] — follows the way out, way through, and way in as the formative journey of God's covenant people

### God With Us — presence and homecoming

- [[The Church Workshop]] — culminates the temple storyline in the Spirit-filled Church as God's living dwelling and a priestly people sent to the world
- Entrance program: [[Building A House]] — trains participants to recognize the architectural frame before tracing God's dwelling places through Scripture

### Thus Says the LORD — revelation and witness

- Future featured workshops should help participants hear the prophetic echoes, encounter Christ as the one Voice, and embody faithful witness.
- Entrance program: *The Five Echoes* — planned interpretive doorway into the prophetic curriculum

## Standalone Garden Project workshops

- [[Christianity as Counterculture]] — the ministry's missional manifesto: inner transformation, Christian community, culture-making, and witness
- [[Forming a Reading Culture]] — forms a community to read with humility, encounter Christ at the center of Scripture, and inhabit the Story together

Standalone does not mean disconnected. These workshops serve the Garden Project's wider mission and may later contribute to a curriculum without being owned by one.

## Workshop design standard

Before a workshop is pilot-ready, it should include:

- audience, prerequisites, duration, capacity, and setting;
- a transformation promise and no more than three major outcomes;
- a biblically outlined and Christ-centered learning arc;
- a minute-by-minute facilitator run-of-show;
- clear relationships to its source curriculum, frameworks, and programs;
- participant materials and accessibility alternatives;
- physical, emotional, pastoral, food, and safeguarding considerations;
- a supply list, staffing plan, and contingency plan;
- prayer, reflection, integration, and a next faithful step;
- pilot evidence, feedback, revision history, provenance, and version number.
