# Grace–Truth Matrix (Simplified)

## Definitions

**Truth**: what is objectively and universally real—facts, reality, God's Word. Truth does not depend on our belief.

**Grace** (*charis*): God's unearned favor—his empowering love given as gift. Grace is received, not achieved.

> 🧭 **Quadrant legend**
>
> I = High Truth + Low Grace (Performance)
> II = Low Truth + Low Grace (Isolation)
> III = Low Truth + High Grace (Total Excess)
> IV = High Truth + High Grace (Grace & Truth)

## Scriptures

- John 1:14 — "...full of grace and truth."
- John 1:17 — "Grace and truth came through Jesus Christ."
- John 8:31–32 — "...you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free."
- John 3:17 — "...not to condemn...but to save..."
- Ephesians 2:8 — "...by grace...it is the gift of God."
- 1 John 1:5–2:1 — walking in the light, confession, cleansing (grace and truth together)

## Connections

Use this section to connect the matrix to what you're seeing in a passage, a relationship, or your own heart.

**A diagnostic lens**

- **Grace axis:** Where is welcome, patience, help, gift, covenant love?
- **Truth axis:** Where is reality named, light exposed, command given, identity clarified?

**Three integrating questions**

1. **What's missing** here—grace, truth, or both?
2. **What's being protected** (reputation, control, comfort, self-justification, autonomy)?
3. **What would movement toward Quadrant IV** look like (honesty + love; repentance + re-engagement)?

**A shorthand map**

- **Grace without truth** → permissive/enslaving ("cheap peace")
- **Truth without grace** → harsh/unsafe (fear, hiding, performance)
- **No grace + no truth** → disconnection and despair
- **Grace + truth** → safe love in reality ("good medicine")

## Applications

### 1) Bible study

- Identify where each character/posture sits (I–IV).
- Name what grace or truth is being withheld.
- Ask what repentance and relational movement toward Quadrant IV would require.

*Example prompts*

- "Where is truth being used without love?"
- "Where is grace being offered without transformation?"

### 2) Stories over time (movement)

Track trajectories, not only locations:

- **Job:** lived experience can feel like **IV → II → IV**; friends often function as **I**; wife can resemble **III**.
- **Prodigal son (Luke 15):** younger son's arc **III → II → IV**; older brother as **I**.

### 3) Wisdom for life (virtue integration)

Many goods are combinations of grace and truth:

- **Love** = grace that tells the truth
- **Boundaries** = truth expressed with grace
- **Forgiveness** = grace that doesn't deny evil and doesn't weaponize it
- **Humility** = truth about self without self-hatred
- **Peace** = relational safety in reality
- **Discipline** = truth aimed at restoration
- **Freedom** = belonging by grace + direction by truth

> 🛠️ **Weekly practice**
>
> Choose one relationship or recurring internal struggle. Name your quadrant. Then ask:
> *What would "more grace" look like here?*
> *What would "more truth" look like here?*
