## Debrief (do not rush)

**Total debrief time:** (e.g., 10–20 min)

### Debrief questions (pick 5–8)

1. What did you feel at the start vs. later?
2. What did you notice about yourself?
3. What did you notice about others / the group?
4. What made this harder or easier?
5. What do you think each element represented?
6. Where does this show up in real life?
7. What might Jesus be inviting you into?
8. What is one next step?

### Facilitator notes

- Start with feelings/observations before interpretation.
- Normalize different responses.
- Keep it non-performative: no "right answer" pressure.
