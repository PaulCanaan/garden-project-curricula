# Leader Notes (Draft v1)

## 1. Session Aim

### Big Idea (fixed)

Eternal life begins now because God's future has entered the present in Christ.

### Central question (draft)

If eternal life is knowing God now (not merely going somewhere later), what changes about how we live in time—this week?

### Formation goal (draft)

Participants begin to practice an "eternity perspective" that produces both:

- **urgency** (mission / love for others)
- **patience** (forgiveness / letting go / presence)

---

## 2. Preparation

### Read (required)

- John 17:3
- Ecclesiastes 3 (with special attention to 3:11)

### Review the source extractions (required)

- Planted. 2025 Week 12 (source extraction appended)
- See The Other Side (source extraction appended)
- Already / Not Yet (EN) (source extraction appended)

### Pastoral awareness (draft)

- Many participants bring an "afterlife-only" model of Christianity. This session aims to correct without mockery, confusion, or overstatement.
- Avoid sweeping claims you cannot defend in-context in the moment; prefer careful, invitational language.

### Leader caution (intermediate state + hope)

This session is **not** denying the intermediate state (the reality that believers who die are with the Lord while awaiting resurrection). The session is resisting a **reduced or escapist** account of Christian hope (as if the gospel were mainly "leaving earth for heaven"). The intended emphasis is:

> **The Christian hope is larger than going to heaven after death. Scripture directs our hope toward life with God, resurrection, and the renewal of heaven and earth.**

---

## 3. Program Guidance

### Purpose

Open the session with a perspective-shift experience that makes participants curious about **what eternal life actually means**.

### Suggested opener options (choose one)

1. **Perspective shift illustration** (from "See The Other Side")
   - Use the "upside down / right side up" framing to introduce the idea that our default assumptions about heaven, eternity, and the Kingdom may need reorientation.
2. **Question hook** (simple)
   - "When you hear 'eternal life,' what do you think Jesus meant?"

### Watch for

- Turning the session into a debate about "going to heaven."
- Making absolute claims (see Human Review flags).

### Approved framing (use in teaching)

Use this approved expression (instead of absolute or dismissive wording):

> **The Christian hope is larger than going to heaven after death. Scripture directs our hope toward life with God, resurrection, and the renewal of heaven and earth.**

### Debrief + transition

Move from the opener into the key claim:

> Jesus defines eternal life in John 17:3.

---

## 4. Framework Guidance

### Framework name (draft)

**Kingdom + Eternity Perspective (Already / Not Yet)**

### Core idea

God's future is not only a far-off destination; in Christ, God's life has entered the present. Christians live in the tension of **already / not yet**.

### Guardrails

- Keep John 17:3 and Ecclesiastes 3 as primary; do not let supporting passages replace them.
- Avoid implying that "already/not yet" solves every question; it is a lens, not the whole map.

### Practice (draft)

Invite one concrete practice for the week:

- "In one situation where you feel hurried, afraid, or stuck, pause and pray: 'Help me value Your presence more than outcomes.'"

---

## 5. Passage Guidance

### A) John 17:3 (primary)

#### Context (to be finalized in review)

Jesus' prayer frames eternal life relationally.

#### Key emphasis (source-grounded)

- Eternal life is not merely infinite duration; it is **knowing** the only true God and Jesus Christ.
- "Knowing" is relational intimacy, not mere information.

#### Likely questions

- "Does that mean heaven doesn't matter?"
- "How can eternal life begin now if we still die?"

### B) Ecclesiastes 3 (primary)

#### Key emphasis (source-grounded)

- Ecclesiastes 3:11 is used in sources as: "God has planted eternity in the human heart."
- Use this as the experiential bridge: people sense longing beyond time.

#### Caution

- The sources focus on 3:11 more than the whole chapter; handle the rest of Ecclesiastes 3 carefully and do not overclaim.

---

## 6. Teaching Emphasis

### Main insight (draft)

Because God's future has entered the present in Christ, eternal life begins now as life with God—reorienting our view of time, presence, and hope.

### Christological center

Hold the session's center here:

- Eternal life is defined by Jesus (John 17:3).
- Eternal life is made possible through Jesus' person and work (stated in sources through the story of Scripture and reconciliation language).

### Supporting storyline connections (from sources; keep secondary)

- God's desire to dwell with God's people (Eden → tabernacle → temple → Spirit → new creation).

## 7. Discussion and Formation

### Essential discussion questions (source-grounded)

1. "How do you understand eternal life?" (explicit in Week 12 source)
2. "What makes heaven 'heaven'?" (prompt used in See The Other Side)
3. "Where do you need a perspective shift this week?"

### Formation applications (source-grounded)

- **Urgency:** eternal perspective awakens love for others and mission.
- **Patience:** in the spectrum of eternity, practice forgiveness and letting go.
- **Prayer:** ask the Holy Spirit to reshape imagination and perspective.

### Weekly practice (draft)

- Choose one relationship or stress point.
- Pray once per day: "Holy Spirit, construct my eternity perspective. Help me see Your presence here."

# Human Review (Decisions + revisions)

- **Review ID:** HR-001
- **Issue Type:** Theological Precision and Pastoral Tone
- **Original wording:** "There's never a statement in the Bible that indicates that we will go to heaven, especially 'after we die'."

- **Decision:** Accepted for revision
- **Reason:** This preserves the source material's critique of escapist framing while using more biblically responsible and pastorally careful language.
