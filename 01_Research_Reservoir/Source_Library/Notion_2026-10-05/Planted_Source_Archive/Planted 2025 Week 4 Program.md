# Planted. 2025 — Week 4 Program: Dartboard

**Purpose of the program:** We don't even need the Bible to tell us, but just by living in this broken world, we can know that we are all broken people, and everyone is broken differently. This program is designed not for condemning us but helping us understand why brokenness is linked with human nature.

**Set up:** Prepare a magnetic dartboard, darts, and some blindfolds like eye masks. (I have them prepared.) Hang the dartboard on the wall. The Program Facilitator can keep the scores to make the activity more fun.

**Instructions:** Each person has 3 shots for each round.

- Round 1: throw darts regularly.
- Round 2: increase the distance.
- Round 3: level up again. Shoot darts with a blindfold.

**Discussion** (<u>really take your time in this, encourage people to engage in conversations</u>):

1. In archery games, what does the referee say if you "miss the mark"?
2. What do you think the bullseye represents?
3. What do you think the distance and eye mask in the program represent?

**Key Points:**

- The bullseye represents the standard of God. Sin is anywhere away from the bullseye.
- The distance represents how close our relationship with God is (an oversimplified depiction). It's easier to follow the Way of God if we build a close relationship with Him.
- Eye covering represents that we were walking in the darkness before we met God, therefore, there's no way that we were able to hit the bullseye of righteousness by our own will power.

**Scriptures (grace & truth):**

- **Romans 3:23** — For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God.
- **Ecclesiastes 7:20** — Indeed, there is no one on earth who is righteous, no one who does what is right and never sins.
- **1 John 1:8** — If we say we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us. (read the whole passage if needed: verses 7–10)
- **John 3:17** — For God did not send his Son into the world to condemn the world, but to save the world through him.
- **Psalm 34:18** — The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.
- **Matthew 5:3** — Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.
