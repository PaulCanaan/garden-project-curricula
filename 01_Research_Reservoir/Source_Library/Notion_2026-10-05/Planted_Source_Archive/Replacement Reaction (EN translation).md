# Replacement Reaction (EN)

*(Chinese source translation — Planted Vol. 4)*

## Activity: Handstand (Perspective Shift)

**Materials:** open space, a wall, yoga mat
**Set up:** place the yoga mat near the wall corner

### Flow

1. Warm up: shoulders, elbows, wrists
2. Learn a wall-walk handstand (get used to being upside down; notice which muscles are engaged)
3. Learn how to come down sideways safely ("learn humility before being lifted up")
4. Learn a tripod headstand (stable triangle; feel the importance of core strength)
5. Practice wall-supported handstand (kick up; hip control through the core)
6. Variations + recommended daily practice movements

### Discussion

- What do you notice and what do you feel when upside down?
- When you handstand, everything looks inverted. So what's upside down—us, or the world?
- Why does the Kingdom Jesus proclaims often feel "upside down" compared to the world?
- Could it be that we have been "upside down" for so long that we've become numb to it?
