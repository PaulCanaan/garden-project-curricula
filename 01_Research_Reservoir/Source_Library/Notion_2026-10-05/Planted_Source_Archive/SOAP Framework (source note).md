# S.O.A.P.

*A framework for reading, studying, and living out the Bible.*

### S – Scripture

What is the literary style of the passage? (narrative, poetry, wisdom literature, prophecy, gospel, epistle)

Slow down and read in context. Pay attention to the key words, grammar, repetition, and structure.

### O – Observation

What is happening in the passage? (who, what, where, when, and why)

What does the passage reveal about God, humanity, and God's relationship with His people?

Where does this passage stand in the story of redemption?

### A – Application

How is God shaping my heart, desires, or character through this passage?

How can I embed this biblical truth into daily rhythms?

### P – Prayer

We are answerable subjects before the Word of God, and prayer is our faithful response.

Praise God for who He is and what He has done.

Ask the Holy Spirit to help me live by faith, grow in obedience, and cultivate hope in Christ.
