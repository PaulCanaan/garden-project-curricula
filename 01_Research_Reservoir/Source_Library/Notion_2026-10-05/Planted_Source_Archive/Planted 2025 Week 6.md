## Other key points – 20 minutes

- **Know the enemy**
  > **Ephesians 6:12 (NLT)** — For we are not fighting against flesh-and-blood enemies, but against evil rulers and authorities in this dark world, and against evil spirits in the heavenly places.
- **Strategies of the enemy** – isolation, whispering lies, manipulating, accusing.
- **Lies are twisted truth**
  > **John 8:44** — When he lies, he speaks his native language, for he is a liar and the father of lies.

  (The devil does not have the ability to create anything bad, he doesn't have anything original, he just takes what God created for the good and makes it meant for evil.)
- **Noble lies** – "God did not give you the best, we can offer better…" but never deliver.
- **The raw materials the devil uses to form weapons**
  > **1 John 2:16** — For everything in the world - the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life - comes not from the Father but from the world.

  |  | The lust of the flesh | The lust of the eyes | The pride of life |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Devil to Eve | the woman saw the fruit of the tree was good for food (Gen 3:6) | (the fruit of the tree was) pleasing to the eye (Gen 3:6) | and also desirable for gaining wisdom (Gen 3:6) |
  | Devil to Jesus | "Tell these stones to become bread." (Matt 4:3) | (the devil) showed him all the kingdoms of the world and their splendor (Matt 4:8) | "All this I will give you…" (Matt 4:9) |

- **Solitude vs. isolation** — Jesus was fasting and praying for 40 days in the presence of his Father, he wasn't alone. His physical body might be weak, but his spirit is stronger than ever.
- **Jesus knows his identity**
  > **Matthew 3:17** — And a voice from heaven said, "This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased."
- Jesus countered the lies with truth: Deuteronomy 8:3, 6:16, 6:13
- The triumph of Jesus over the devil was not simply casting out the existence of the devil but disarming him.
- Psalm 91 – the enemy also knows the Scripture, but he can't change his destiny.
- **Battle vs. war**
  > **Luke 4:13** — When the devil had finished all this tempting, he left him until an opportune time.
- Jesus redeems what has been stolen by the devil, resurrects who has been killed, and restores the destroyed. He speaks truths to the lies. He brings identities over the shame. Forgiveness to the guilt. Home and community for the isolated and the outcast.
- **Hospital for sinners**
  > **Matthew 9:12–13** — It's not the healthy who need a doctor, but the sick… For I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners.

  (You don't clean yourself before you turn back to God, just like you don't wait to see the doctor till you are healed. Healing is always available in the name of Jesus. Even for the healthy, no one can promise they won't be sick in their lifetime, just like no one can say they won't sin anymore. Sin is a contagious disease, but the Good News is: Jesus died for our sins from the past, present, and in the future. It doesn't mean we can keep messing around, Jesus loves us too much to let us stay the same. Who would like to be sick and broken when they could choose to be healthy and whole?)

## Homework for our meeting 2 weeks later

- Prepare to share your testimony.
- Since all of us will share, we need to limit each sharing time under 5 minutes. This will help – when you prepare for it, try to write down your testimony, read it and record it, see how long it's going take.
- In your testimony, think about these three questions:
  1. What was your life like before you accepted Jesus as your Lord and Savior?
  2. The moment you experienced Jesus in your life. What was it like?
  3. What is your life now? How has Jesus been working and changing your life?
- Often God reveals the details when you recall them and write them down. Pray for authenticity and courage. Be as vulnerable as you can.
