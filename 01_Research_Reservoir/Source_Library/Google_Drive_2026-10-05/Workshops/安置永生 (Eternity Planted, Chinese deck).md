# 安置永生

> [!note] English summary (added at import, not part of the source)
> A Chinese-language deck for Planted. Chapter 4 on Ecclesiastes 3:11, "He has set eternity in the human heart". It moves through:
> a perspective shift to the "already / not yet" Kingdom (Colossians 3:1-4; Philippians 3:10-14; the BibleProject on heaven and earth);
> eternal life as life with God rather than "going to heaven after you die" (with a discussion question); a finite / eternal /
> infinite time diagram with Augustine (*Confessions*) and Psalm 90:2; what Scripture says about eternity (Ecclesiastes 3:11; John
> 17:2-3; 2 Corinthians 4:18; 1 John 5:11), concluding that eternal life is life with God beyond the limits of time; living in God's
> story (Christopher J. H. Wright's seven-part storyline and the five-part structure of Planted., after Bridges International's
> *A Hero's Story*); "practising eternity" (William Blake) through Ecclesiastes 3:1-15; the "redefined YOLO" (You Only Live Once,
> Live It For Eternity); the five frameworks and the Grace–Truth Matrix; Growth = (Truth + Grace) × Time with 1 Corinthians 3:6-7;
> and Matthew 28:18-20, pointing to the next chapter, 逆行者 ("against the current"). See the English deck for the parallel text.

## 你对永生的理解是什么样的？

耶稣所应许的不是“叫一切信他的死后能上天堂”，而是“不至灭亡，反得永生”。天堂并不是一个你死后所去的“地方”，而是神的同在在哪里，哪里就是天堂。 一方面我们容易陷入逃避主义，另一方面我们常常会爱礼物胜过送礼物的人。追寻天堂，你会错失和神的关系；追寻神，你反而能得到天堂。信仰不是到达某种目的的一种手段，而是目的本身。和基督的关系是我们信仰生命唯一且最好的的奖赏。如果这份信仰仅仅是为了一个“来世的地方”，那就是买椟还珠，本末倒置了。

永生 > “死后上天堂”
