## Slide 4 — How do you understand the eternal life?

HOW DO YOU UNDERSTAND THE ETERNAL LIFE?

What Jesus offers is not a ticket to heaven, but the eternal life. Heaven is not “a place where you go after you die” in a sense of escapism, but where the presence of God is. We tend to love gifts more than the Giver. Aiming the heaven rather than the One who makes heaven heaven would be the wrong focus. Believing in Jesus is not a means to an end, it is its own end. The only and the best reward of our faith is the relationship with the personal God, not just a place for our “afterlife”.

ETERNAL LIFE > “GO TO HEAVEN AFTER YOU DIE”
