## Slide 2 — What is Planted. Bible Study?

*Planted. 2026*

Planted. is a seeker- and new-believer-friendly journey through the biblical story and the life of following Jesus. Moving from creation to new creation, it invites participants to see Scripture as one coherent story centered on Christ—and to locate their own lives within that story.

Biblically outlined, Christ-centered, and formation-oriented, Planted. weaves together Scripture, biblical theology, experiential learning, conversation, prayer, worship, and community. Through five chapters and five recurring frameworks, participants are invited not only to understand the Christian faith, but to inhabit it.

Like a seed planted in the ground, faith is meant to take root, rise into new life, grow over time, bear fruit, and plant again.

## Slide 3 — Formation Goal

*Planted. 2026*

Our prayer is that participants will:

- be rooted in grace and truth;
- learn to hear God through Scripture;
- see their story within God’s story;
- behold and follow Jesus;
- grow in faith over time;
- belong to the people of God;
- plant seeds of hope in the lives of others.

The goal is not merely to produce informed Christians, but to cultivate people who are increasingly formed into the likeness of Christ.

## Slide 4 — Journey Roadmap

Each gathering weaves together several forms of learning and formation:

- **Scripture** — reading and studying the biblical text
- **Story** — locating each passage within the larger story of God
- **Experience** — activities, visual metaphors, art, and embodied learning
- **Conversation** — honest questions and shared discovery
- **Practice** — applying truth in everyday life
- **Prayer and worship** — responding to God personally and together
- **Community** — growing alongside others rather than alone

## Slide 5 — Orientation Session: The Seed and the Soil

*Planted. 2026*

A seed may look small and ordinary, but it carries life within it. Once it enters the soil, something hidden begins. Roots reach downward before anything appears above the ground. Growth comes slowly, quietly, and often invisibly.

**let’s tend the soil**

Session 0 is an orientation session. Before we move into Genesis and the story of the garden, we begin with Jesus’ parable about soil— because the same seed can be received in very different ways.

## Slide 6 — The Parable of the Sower

*Planted. 2026*

[Matthew 13:3-9] Then he told them many things in parables, saying, “Consider the sower who went out to sow. As he sowed, some seed fell along the path, and the birds came and devoured them. Other seed fell on rocky ground where it didn’t have much soil, and it grew up quickly since the soil wasn’t deep. But when the sun came up, it was scorched, and since it had no root, it withered away. Other seed fell among thorns, and the thorns came up and choked it. Still other seed fell on good ground and produced fruit: some a hundred, some sixty, and some thirty times what was sown. Let anyone who has ears listen.”

## Slide 7 — Program: Relationships

*Planted. 2026*

*[Import note: the PDF text also contains the fragment "The Four" on this slide; the program is The Grid.]*

The room is set up as a large square divided into four quadrants. Three quadrants contain different activities, while the fourth is intentionally left empty.

For the next 20 minutes, you may move between the three active quadrants anytime you want—but wherever you are, you must keep doing that quadrant’s activity.

- **Top left:** burpees or jumping jacks with perfect form and a perfect smile.
- **Bottom left:** sit or squat, turn away, cover your ears, and disengage.
- **Bottom right:** enjoy unlimited snacks and drinks, but you must keep eating or drinking while you stay there.

Try staying in one quadrant for at least five minutes.

When time is up, stop what you’re doing and move together into the empty top- right quadrant. Once you’re there, you don’t have to do anything. Just rest.

## Slide 8 — Framework: The Grace-Truth Matrix

*Planted. 2026*

**Truth:** what is objectively and universally real—facts, reality, God’s Word. Truth does not depend on our belief.

**Grace (charis):** God’s unearned favor—His empowering love given as gift. Grace is received, not achieved.

[John 1:14] The Word became flesh and dwelt among us. We observed his glory, the glory as the one and only Son from the Father, full of grace and truth.

> “Truth without grace is mean, grace without truth is meaningless, but truth and grace together are good medicine.” – Chris Hodges

> “To be loved but not known is comforting but superficial. To be known and not loved is our greatest fear. But to be fully known and truly loved is, well, a lot like being loved by God. It is what we need more than anything. It liberates us from pretense, humbles us out of our self-righteousness, and fortifies us for any difficulty life can throw at us.” – Timothy Keller

## Slide 10 — Entering the Story

*Planted. 2026*

The story of Planted. begins in a garden.

Humanity is created for life, relationship, and good purpose. Yet sin brings darkness, alienation, and exile. Jesus enters the broken world, passes through suffering and death, and rises as the firstfruits of new creation.

The seed that seemed buried was planted.

Through the life of Jesus, faith takes root. Through grace and truth over time, it grows. A tree does not remain alone; trees become a forest. The forest bears fruit, and the fruit carries seeds.

This is the journey ahead:

God planted a garden out of delight; humanity made to flourish; exile east of Eden; the faithful Son who crushed the serpent; the Seed who must die; the Firstfruits who rise; the Gospel of the Kingdom; eternity planted in the present; a forest growing together; and a people sent to plant again.

No one enters this story with perfect soil.

We enter with questions, wounds, hopes, resistance, faith, doubt, and unfinished stories. The invitation is not to pretend otherwise. It is to come honestly and allow the Gardener to work.
