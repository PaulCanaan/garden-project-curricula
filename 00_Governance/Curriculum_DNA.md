# Curriculum DNA

The Garden Project curricula are governed by three design principles.

## 1. Biblically outlined

Each curriculum should derive its architecture from Scripture's storyline,
literary form, canonical movement, themes, and textual patterns.

## 2. Christ-centered

Each curriculum should reveal how biblical stories, covenants, institutions,
promises, patterns, and hopes reach their climax and fulfillment in Jesus Christ.

## 3. Formation-oriented

Each curriculum should move beyond information transfer toward worship,
identity, character, wisdom, embodied practice, community, and mission.
