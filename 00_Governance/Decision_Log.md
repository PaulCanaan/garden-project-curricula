## HR-001 — Heaven after death (Planted. Session 8, *Eternity Planted*)

- **Date:** 2026-08-04
- **Reviewer:** Paul
- **Status:** Approved
- **Issue type:** Theological precision and pastoral tone
- **Original wording:** "There's never a statement in the Bible that indicates that we will go to heaven, especially 'after we die'."
- **Concern:** The wording is too absolute. The intended correction is valid, but the statement may overreach and cause unnecessary confusion.
- **Approved canonical expression:** "The Christian hope is larger than going to heaven after death. Scripture directs our hope toward life with God, resurrection, and the renewal of heaven and earth."
- **Reason:** It keeps the source's critique of escapist framing while using more biblically responsible and pastorally careful language.
- **Affects:** Session overview, teaching outline, leader cautions, discussion questions.
