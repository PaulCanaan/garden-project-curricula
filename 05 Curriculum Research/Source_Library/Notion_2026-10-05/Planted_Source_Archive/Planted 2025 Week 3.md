# Planted. 2025 — Week 3: Humanity

**Part 1: devotional Bible study on Psalm 139 (40 minutes).**

## Planted. week 2 homework

1. In what ways are Adam and Eve like the other creatures in Genesis 1 and 2, and in what ways are they different?
2. What does God's command and blessing to humans (Genesis 1:28, 2:15) make us think about working and calling?

### Genesis 1

(v. 26) Then God said, "Let us make mankind in our image, in our likeness, so that they may rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky, over the livestock and all the wild animals, and over all the creatures that move along the ground."

(v. 28) God blessed them and said to them, "Be fruitful and increase in number; fill the earth and subdue it. Rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky and over every living creature that moves on the ground."

### Genesis 2

(v. 7) Then the Lord God formed a man (*āḏām*) from the dust of the ground (*ăḏāmâ*) and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and the man became a living being.

(v. 8) Now the Lord God had planted a garden in the east, in Eden; and there he put the man he had formed.

(v. 15) The Lord God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden to work it and take care of it.

(v. 23) The man said,
"This is now bone of my bones
and flesh of my flesh;
she shall be called 'woman,'
for she was taken out of man."

(v. 25) Adam and his wife were both naked, and they felt no shame.

- Adam – man, humanity
- Eve – to live, life
- Eden – pleasure or delight

- Image-bearers of God. Image (*ṣelem*) means statue, likeness. We are visible representative of an invisible God.
- Dust means mortality in the Hebrew context. Humans were mere dusts, and we all get limitations, but God made beautiful things out of dust. We need to learn to embrace our limitations and hold on to the potentials God put in us.
- Bone of bones, flesh of fleshes – how life is intertwined with humanity, life is ultimately from the breath of life of God.
- Having dominion/rule over – kingdom/royal languages – kings and queens
- Subdue – to draw the potential out of something.

## There Is a Garden Named Delight

**Proverbs 8:30–31** — Then I was constantly at his side. I was filled with delight day after day, rejoicing always in his presence, rejoicing in his whole world and delighting in mankind.

**Zephaniah 3:17** — The Lord your God is with you, the Mighty Warrior who saves. He will take great delight in you; in his love he will no longer rebuke you, but will rejoice over you with singing.

**Psalm 8:4–5** — what is mankind that you are mindful of them, human beings that you care for them? You have made them a little lower than the angels and crowned them with glory and honor.

- Intimate relationship with God is like seeds being delightfully planted in the garden.

## We are a seed

**1 Peter 1:23** — For you have been born again, not of perishable seed, but of imperishable, through the living and enduring word of God.

- Farmers don't grieve over the seed they just sowed because they know how the seeds will end up being, so they picture the harvest and sow with joy.
- "Be fruitful and multiply." It's gardener's language. God is the Ultimate Gardener. He tends the garden of our soul.
- We are valued because we are loved by God. God prepared a garden of delight for His creation, for us, it's not because of how great we are, but because of how much He loved us.

**Fun Activity – drawing a seed planted in a beautiful garden**

**Feeling small and significant at the same time – the sense of awe and wonder.**

- "Fill the earth and subdue it." Who are we but God chose to partner with us to bring forth His mission on earth?
- "Eden was unspoiled but also unfinished." – Nancy Guthrie, *Even Better Than Eden*

**Matthew 17:20** — "If you have faith as small as a mustard seed, you can say to this mountain, 'Move from here to there,' and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you."

- It's not the tiny mustard seeds (us) that are powerful, but the One who we put faith into. Greater in the One who is in me than the one in the world.

## A Prayer I wrote down when I was writing this chapter

Dear Heavenly Father,
thank you for creating us fearfully and wonderfully in your own image.
This is too wonderful, it's incomprehensible, that You read our heart like an open book. And You signed Your name on it.
This book that is not written with pen and ink but written with your living Spirit.
Publish us to the world, so that people can see the Author through us. Make us your proud, as we live out Your will.
Remind us that we were mere dust, help us to accept our limitations, we can only be at one place at a time, but because of You, every moment is significant.
Thank you for Your breath of life, we came alive, and Your Spirit dwells inside of us.
Help us to embrace the potentials you already put inside of us. We are image-bearers of You, God. We are known as children of God by reflecting Your characters.
We long to grow in your truth and grace. As a seed, we are so small, but you prepared a garden of delight for us to have relationship with You. We don't deserve it, can't earn it. It's not because how good we are, but because how much You love us.
I stand before You in awe and wonder. When I face the mountains, I will know You are the Maker. It's not me who can move the mountains, but the faith that I have in You makes nothing impossible.
I can't thank You enough, Lord my God. I praise You in your beautiful, wonderful, and powerful Name, Amen.
