# Planted. 2025 — Week 9: The Second Adam

**Opening Prayer**

**Text:** Luke 22; 1 Corinthians 15 (a little bit of Romans 15 and 2 Corinthians 5)

## Key points

- Breakdown is the beginning of breakthrough.
- In a garden, in front of a tree, disobedience vs. obedience
- the heart of resurrection in the NT, echoing Genesis 1–3
- Jesus, the first fruit
- not re-creation, but new creation. (all new things vs. all things new)
- Resurrection is not a metaphysical perspective ("physical body" vs. "spiritual body"), but an eternal perspective (present body vs. future body).

**Book Club:** *Surprised By Hope* by N. T. Wright

**Framework 3:** The Gospel Flow
