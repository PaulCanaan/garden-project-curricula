## Homework from last week

- What was really going on there?
- What does God's reaction tell us about who He is?

**Text:** Genesis 3 (Discovery Bible studies)

**Questions:** Where is sin from? What are the consequences of sin?

## Key points

- The craftiness (sounds like Hebrew word for naked). Father of the lies – lies are twisted truths
- The desire of the eyes, the desire of the flesh, the pride of life – which are from the world
- Three enemies – the devil, the flesh, the world. The battlefields – Truth vs. lies, Spirit vs. the flesh, Kingdom vs. the world
- Tree of knowledge of good and evil – autonomy & self-justifying – choosing our own way instead of the life-giving wisdom of God
- Temptation (lies from the enemy) is different from sin, but only when we say yes and continue to say yes to the temptation, it becomes sin. Jesus was tempted in all ways, but He never sinned. He didn't say yes to the temptation.
- Punishments or consequences?
- The promised Hope
  > The covering and the sacrifice behind it
- The boundary God set between the Tree of Life and the fallen humanity is an immense act of grace – it keeps spiritual death from eternal death/separation.
