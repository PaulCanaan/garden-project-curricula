# Program: The Grid (The Four Relationships of Grace and Truth)

**Session:** Introduction Session - The Seed and the Soil
**Estimated Time:** 15–25 min activity + 20–30 min discussion

---

## Purpose

This program helps participants experience:

> How we instinctively respond to different types of relationships—through performance, isolation, or indulgence—and how the way of Jesus (grace + truth) forms a different kind of freedom.

The program is designed to prepare participants to explore:

- How grace and truth show up (or fail to show up) in our relationships
- The difference between being *loved but not known* vs. *known but not loved* vs. *fully known and truly loved*
- Our default strategies for self-protection (earning, hiding, numbing)
- The invitation of the gospel: honest self-knowledge held inside real love

The activity should not attempt to teach the whole session. Its purpose is to create a shared experience that the group can later interpret through Scripture and the session framework.

---

## Desired Discovery

Participants may begin to notice that:

> Our default responses can temporarily relieve discomfort, but they cannot heal us. The "empty quadrant" is not empty because it is impossible—it is empty because it is not our instinct. Being fully known and truly loved is received, practiced, and sustained by grace and truth.

Do not explain this before the activity unless safety or clarity requires it. Allow participants to feel each quadrant long enough for patterns to surface.

---

## Materials

- Rope / string / tape (enough to mark a large 2×2 grid)
- Snacks + drinks (preferably something sweet + cheesy + soda)

### Optional

- Paper signs for each quadrant
- Phone timer + music
- Wet wipes / trash bag

---

## Setup

Before participants arrive:

1. Use ropes (or tape) to form a large square divided into 4 quadrants (a visible "matrix").
2. Label each quadrant:
   - **Top left (Quadrant I):** Performance (earning love / acceptance)
   - **Bottom left (Quadrant II):** Isolation (shutdown / hiding)
   - **Bottom right (Quadrant III):** Total Excess (cheap comfort / numbing)
   - **Top right (Quadrant IV — reveal later):** Grace & Truth (real relationship)
3. Place snacks + drinks next to the **bottom right** quadrant.
4. Ensure there is enough room for movement and that people won't trip over ropes.

Arrange the room so that:

- participants can move between quadrants safely;
- observers can stand outside the grid and still engage.

---

## Facilitator Preparation

The Program Facilitator should understand:

- the goal is to surface patterns, not to embarrass people;
- each quadrant should feel "costly" in a different way;
- the discussion must move from experience → observation → interpretation → response;
- the goal is not to label people, but to name patterns.

Before beginning, decide:

- your time signals (every 5 minutes is helpful);
- movement alternatives for burpees/jumping jacks;
- allergy / dietary notes;
- boundaries for snack-tossing (gentle only; no faces).

---

## Introduction to Participants

Give only the information needed to participate:

> "We're going to do a 20-minute experience in a grid. Each quadrant has a different 'way of being.' You can switch quadrants anytime you want, but you can't take breaks from the activity in your quadrant. Notice what happens in you as you move—and as you stay."

Explain:

- Total duration: ~20 minutes.
- Freedom: switch quadrants anytime.
- Constraint: no breaks inside a quadrant.
- Safety: no mocking; participation is invitational.

Avoid explaining what each quadrant symbolizes.

---

## Instructions

### Preparation

1. Walk the group around the grid and briefly show what happens in each quadrant.
2. Explain the core rule: "Wherever you are, you must keep doing the activity of that quadrant."
3. Encourage participants to stay in a quadrant for >5 minutes at least once.

### Quadrant Activities

#### Top Left — Performance / Perfectionism

- Do burpees *or* jumping jacks.
- Requirement: **perfect form** and a **perfect smile**.
- Accessibility option: step-jacks, wall pushups, marching in place.

#### Bottom Left — Isolation / Shutdown

- Squat or sit down.
- Turn away from everyone.
- Close eyes, cover ears.
- Say phrases like: "I'm not listening." "I don't care." "Leave me alone."
- Accessibility option: sit in a chair with head down, hands over ears.

#### Bottom Right — Total Excess / Indulgence

- Unlimited snacks and drinks.
- You must keep eating/drinking while you're in the quadrant.
- You may toss snacks to other groups to distract them *only if* it's gentle and respectful.
- Safety: no hard throws; no faces; respect allergies.

#### Top Right — Grace & Truth (Reveal Space)

- Not used during the 20-minute "no break" portion.
- At the end, everyone comes here to rest.

### Completion

At the end of ~20 minutes:

1. Stop the activity.
2. Invite everyone to move into the **top-right quadrant**.
3. Let the group rest for 60–90 seconds. Provide water.

---

## Accessibility and Safety

- Offer low-impact movement options.
- Check for food allergies / dietary needs.
- No shaming about fitness, food, or social comfort.
- Observing counts as participation.

---

## Discussion

### 1. Experience

1. How do you feel right now?
2. How did you feel when you started in a new quadrant vs. staying there for a while?
3. Which quadrant was most uncomfortable? Which was most attractive?

### 2. Observation

1. Where did you go first—and what drew you there?
2. What did each quadrant do for you (relief, control, distraction, approval)?
3. What did each quadrant cost you (exhaustion, loneliness, shame, numbness)?

### 3. Interpretation

1. What do you think each quadrant represents?
2. Follow-up: In what relationships do you tend to respond in what way? (parents / friends / boss / coworkers / strangers)
3. What do the **x-axis** and the **y-axis** represent?
4. When we finally reveal it: what do you think the **top-right quadrant** represents? Why is it "empty" at first?

### 4. Response

1. In which relationships do you tend to "perform," "hide," or "numb out"?
2. What would it look like to practice grace + truth with one person this week?
3. Which "way out" do you most need right now: **confess**, **connect**, or **concede control**?

---

## Key Points

- **Quadrant I — Performance:** "truth without grace" (rules, earning, self-justification). Way out: **confess**. *(1 John 1:8; Romans 3:28)*
- **Quadrant II — Isolation:** refusing both grace and truth (hiding, shutdown). Way out: **connect**. *(Proverbs 18:1)*
- **Quadrant III — Total Excess:** counterfeit grace (comfort without truth; numbing; slavery). Way out: **concede control**. *(John 8:34; Genesis 3:5)*
- **Quadrant IV — Grace & Truth:** real relationship is being **fully known** and **truly loved**. *(John 1:17; 1 John 1:5–2:1)*

### Central Insight

> The gospel creates a new space: where truth can be told without losing love.

### Important Clarification

> This program does not label people. It helps participants name *patterns* so they can receive grace and practice truth.

### Closing Quote

"To be loved but not known is comforting but superficial. To be known and not loved is our greatest fear. But to be fully known and truly loved is, well, a lot like being loved by God. It is what we need more than anything. It liberates us from pretense, humbles us out of our self-righteousness, and fortifies us for any difficulty life can throw at us."
― Timothy Keller
