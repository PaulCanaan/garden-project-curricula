# Planted. — Overview (Notion)

## Overview

*Planted.* is a seeker- and new-believer-friendly journey through the biblical story and the life of following Jesus. Moving from creation to new creation, it invites participants to see Scripture as one coherent story centered on Christ—and to locate their own lives within that story.

Biblically outlined, Christ-centered, and formation-oriented, *Planted.* weaves together Scripture, biblical theology, experiential learning, conversation, prayer, worship, and community. Through five chapters and five recurring frameworks, participants are invited not only to understand the Christian faith, but to inhabit it.

Like a seed planted in the ground, faith is meant to take root, rise into new life, grow over time, bear fruit, and plant again.

## Formation Goal (summary)

Our prayer is that participants will:

- be rooted in grace and truth;
- learn to hear God through Scripture;
- see their story within God's story;
- behold and follow Jesus;
- grow in faith over time;
- belong to the people of God;
- plant seeds of hope in the lives of others.

The goal is not merely to produce informed Christians, but to cultivate people who are increasingly formed into the likeness of Christ.

## Storyline

**The over-arching storyline of the Bible**: ⭐️ Creation → ⚡️ Fall → ✝️ Redemption → 👑 New Creation → ❓ The mission of the Church. How do we respond to our calling?

**Jesus re-lived the Story of the Bible**: ⭐️ Incarnation → ⚡️ Wilderness temptation → ✝️ Crucifixion → 👑 Resurrection → ❓ How do we respond to the lordship of Jesus?

## Leading *Planted.*

### Leader Guide and Co-Journer Introduction

*Planted.* is not primarily a classroom where an expert delivers information. It is a shared journey in which leaders help participants encounter Scripture, behold Jesus, locate their lives within God's story, and take faithful next steps.

Leaders in *Planted.* are called **co-journers**—companions who walk alongside others in their faith journey. A co-journer does not lead from a position of having arrived, but from a posture of humility, hospitality, curiosity, and faithful discipleship.

Co-journers cultivate an environment where seekers, new believers, returning Christians, and mature disciples can learn together. They welcome honest questions, explain Christian language without assumption, guide participants back to Scripture, and create space for the Holy Spirit to work through conversation, prayer, worship, experience, and community.

A co-journer seeks to:

- welcome people as they are;
- listen before answering;
- invite participation without forcing vulnerability;
- draw people into Scripture rather than simply supply conclusions;
- keep Jesus at the center of every gathering;
- connect biblical truth with lived experience;
- help participants discern faithful next steps;
- model a life rooted in grace and truth.

The goal is not to cover every page or answer every question. It is to help people **inhabit the story**—to see God, themselves, others, and the world through the story Scripture tells.

The leader's work is to prepare the soil, cultivate the environment, and sow faithfully. Growth belongs to God.

## Journey Roadmap

### The Formation Rhythm — *What Participants Can Expect*

Each gathering weaves together several forms of learning and formation:

- **Scripture** — reading and studying the biblical text
- **Story** — locating each passage within the larger story of God
- **Experience** — activities, visual metaphors, art, and embodied learning
- **Conversation** — honest questions and shared discovery
- **Practice** — applying truth in everyday life
- **Prayer and worship** — responding to God personally and together
- **Community** — growing alongside others rather than alone

Five recurring frameworks accompany the journey. They are introduced gradually, practiced together, and revisited as participants' understanding deepens.

### Session Flow — *What Leaders Facilitate*

| Session Flow | Purpose and Practices |
|---|---|
| **Welcome** | Hospitality and relational check-in; recap of the previous gathering; opening question, story, image, or experiential program; introduction to the central tension. |
| **Explore** | Reading the biblical text; observation and discussion; biblical-theological connections; Christ-centered discovery; framework introduction or practice; locating participants' lives within the story. |
| **Debrief** | Personal reflection; practical next step; prayer and worship; preparation for the next gathering; continuation into everyday life. |

Leaders may adapt the timing and activities to the needs of the group, while preserving the formative movement from encounter, to understanding, to faithful response.

## More About Planted.

**Planted. is a seeker- and new-believer-friendly journey through the biblical story and the life of following Jesus.**

Beginning in the garden of creation and moving through human rebellion, the life, death, and resurrection of Jesus, the hope of God's kingdom, and the mission of the church, *Planted.* helps participants see Scripture as one coherent story centered on Christ. Its five chapters follow the movement of a seed: planted in delight, buried in darkness, raised into new life, growing toward the light, and eventually becoming part of a fruit-bearing forest.

Rather than assuming extensive biblical knowledge, the curriculum creates an accessible entrance into Christian faith through Scripture, visual frameworks, experiential programs, honest conversation, prayer, worship, and life together. It is designed for people who are exploring Christianity, beginning their faith journey, returning to faith, or seeking stronger foundations.

*Planted.* is built around three design commitments: **biblically outlined**, **Christ-centered**, and **formation-oriented** (text identical to the Designing Principles source; see [Designing Principles](../../Design_Principles/Designing%20Principles%20(Notion).md)).

The curriculum's five frameworks serve as companions throughout this journey:

- **The Grace–Truth Matrix** cultivates a life rooted in both grace and truth.
- **S.O.A.P.** teaches participants to read, understand, and respond to Scripture.
- **The Gospel Flow** connects God's character and work to Christian identity and action.
- **Know · Grow · Go** gives a simple pattern for lifelong discipleship.
- **PLANT** equips participants to share the hope of Jesus naturally and faithfully.

These frameworks are not presented as isolated tools. They unfold as the curriculum progresses, gaining depth as participants grow. The Grace–Truth Matrix, for example, begins as a lens for relationships and discernment and later develops into a formation model in which growth takes place through grace and truth over time.

Because of its accessible structure and repeatable formation process, *Planted.* can serve churches, campus ministries, small groups, and other Christian communities as a recurring pathway for welcoming seekers, grounding new believers, and helping participants take their next faithful step with Jesus.

### Formation Goal (full)

The goal of *Planted.* is not merely that participants finish a curriculum, but that they begin to **inhabit the story of God**.

By the end of the journey, our prayer is that participants will be beginning to:

#### Be rooted in grace and truth

Receive their identity from the God who knows them, loves them, and calls them into abundant life. They will learn to recognize the distortions of performance, indulgence, and isolation, and to pursue the integrated life revealed in Jesus, who is full of grace and truth.

#### Hear God through Scripture

Approach the Bible with curiosity, humility, and confidence. Through the S.O.A.P. framework, participants will learn to read Scripture in context, observe what it reveals about God and humanity, respond in faithful practice, and turn their reading into prayer.

#### See their story within God's story

Understand the gospel not merely as a formula for personal salvation, but as the good news of what God has done in Christ to restore creation, reconcile humanity, and establish his kingdom. Participants will begin to interpret their identity, suffering, hope, relationships, and calling through this larger story.

#### Behold and follow Jesus

Grow in love for Jesus by encountering his character, teaching, suffering, death, resurrection, reign, and mission. The desired response is not only belief about Jesus, but allegiance to him, imitation of his way, and participation in his life.

#### Grow over time

Recognize that spiritual formation is neither instant nor solitary. Like a seed becoming a tree, growth takes place through grace and truth, practiced faithfully over time, within the presence of God and the life of Christian community.

#### Belong to a people

Move from an individual understanding of faith toward life in the body of Christ. The seed becomes a tree, and the trees become a forest: a community that bears fruit, supports one another, and reflects the presence of God together.

#### Plant seeds of hope

Take meaningful next steps in discipleship and mission—praying, listening, sharing the gospel, serving others, joining Christian community, and helping others encounter Jesus. Participants are invited not only to receive life, but to become people through whom God cultivates life in the world.

### Spiritual Formation Rhythm

> Spiritual formation does not happen through information alone. *Planted.* creates several overlapping environments in which participants can encounter and respond to truth.

- **Scripture** — Participants read, observe, interpret, and respond to biblical texts.
- **Story** — Individual passages are located within the larger story centered on Jesus.
- **Experience** — Programs, visual metaphors, movement, art, and shared activities make abstract truths tangible.
- **Conversation** — Participants learn by listening, questioning, articulating, and reflecting together.
- **Practice** — Each session invites concrete responses in habits, relationships, discipleship, and mission.
- **Prayer and worship** — Participants respond to God rather than merely discussing him.
- **Community** — Growth occurs among people who know, encourage, challenge, forgive, and support one another.

> **Do not rush from experience to explanation.** Allow participants to observe, feel, wonder, and articulate what they notice before supplying the lesson's meaning.
