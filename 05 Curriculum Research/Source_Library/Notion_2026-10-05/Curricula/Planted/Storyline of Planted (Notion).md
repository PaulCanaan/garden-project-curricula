# Storyline of Planted.

![Planted cover tree](notion-attachment: Planted_cover_tree.png — not downloaded; see Notion page)

> This image of planting a dead seed and raising a live plant is a mere sketch at best, but perhaps it will help in approaching the mystery of the resurrection body—but only if you keep in mind that when we're raised, we're raised for *good*, alive forever! The corpse that's planted is no beauty, but when it's raised, it's glorious. Put in the ground weak, it comes up powerful. The seed sown is natural; the seed grown is supernatural—same seed, same body, but what a difference from when it goes down in physical mortality to when it is raised up in spiritual immortality!
> (1 Corinthians 15:42–44, MSG)

## Chapters

1. **There Is a Garden Named Delight** — You are a seed created by God, placed in the garden God prepared for you.
2. **Devil Not Today** — Because of sin and death, you were buried in darkness.
3. **Breakdown Is the Beginning of Breakthrough** — Jesus' death and resurrection brought the hope for life, you start to sprout.
4. **See the Other Side** — You were not buried, you were planted.
5. **From Planted To Plant** — Watered by grace and truth over time, the seed grows into a tree. Eventually there's a forest.
