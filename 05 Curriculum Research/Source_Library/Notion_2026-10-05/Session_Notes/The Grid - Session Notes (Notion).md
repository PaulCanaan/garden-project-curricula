# The Grid — Session Notes

## Pursuing Real Relationships In A Broken World

### 1. The Four Quadrants/Relationships

#### Quadrant I — Performance

*How do you feel when you are trying to earn love and acceptance?*

- Stressed
- Afraid of being exposed
- Tired
- Self-righteous

#### Quadrant II — Isolation

*How do you feel when you are isolated?*

- Safe
- Lonely
- Depressed
- Hopeless

#### Quadrant III — Total Excess

*How do you feel when you abandon your standards and do what you want?*

- Free (at first)
- Empty
- Trapped
- Guilty

#### Quadrant IV (reveal this quadrant later)

*This is where real relationships happen.*

### 2. Analysis of the Four Quadrants

#### Performance

*(1 John 1:8, Romans 3:28)*

- Rules, no grace
- Fake relationships
- Way out: **confess**

#### Isolation

*(Proverbs 18:1)*

- No truth or grace
- No relationships
- Way out: **connect**

#### Total Excess

*(John 8:34, Genesis 3:5)*

- Cheap grace
- Selfish relationships
- Way out: **concede control**

#### Grace & Truth

- Grace: Acceptance of the whole you, the good and bad.
  *John 1:17 "Grace and Truth came through Jesus Christ."*
- Truth: Facts, reality, and the Word of God.
  *It's not about being perfect but being real with God and others. (1 John 1:5–2:1)*
- True grace
- Real relationships

*How can you move towards Grace and Truth?*

### 3. Dive Deeper

#### Definitions

- **Truth:** objectively, universally, what is true in any matter under consideration. Of a truth, in reality, in fact, certainly.
  Truth does not depend on whether we believe it or not.
- **Grace:** *charis* in Greek. The divine influence upon the heart and its reflection in life – acceptable, benefit, favor, gift, joy, liberation, pleasure, thanks.
  Grace is something we don't deserve, we can't earn, but freely receive from God.

#### Scriptures

- **John 1:14** — The Word became flesh and made his dwelling among us. We have seen his glory, the glory of the one and only Son, who came from the Father, full of grace and truth.
- **John 8:31–32 (NLT)** — Jesus said to the people who believed in him, "You are truly my disciples if you remain faithful to my teachings. And you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free."
- **John 18:37b** — "For this purpose I was born and for this purpose I have come into the world—to bear witness to the truth. Everyone who is of the truth listens to my voice."
- **John 3:17** — For God did not send his Son into the world to condemn the world, but to save the world through him.
- **Ephesians 2:8 (NLT)** — God saved you by his grace when you believed. And you can't take credit for this; it is a gift from God.
- **1 Corinthians 13:2** — If I have the gift of prophecy and can fathom all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have a faith that can move mountains, but do not have love, I am nothing.

#### Connections

- "Truth without grace is mean, grace without truth is meaningless, but truth and grace together are good medicine." – Chris Hodges
- Truth is seeing the good, the bad, the ugly; grace is embracing them.
- In a relative world, everyone seems to have their own "truth", but there is an ultimate truth—God's unchanging love for you.
- Grace meets you where you are, but it does not just leave you there. God loves you too much to let you stay the same.
- Truth counts the cost; grace pays it.
- Truth sets us free; grace invites us into that freedom.
