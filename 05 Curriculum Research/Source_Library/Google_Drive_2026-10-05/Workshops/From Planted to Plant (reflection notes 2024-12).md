**From Planted to Plant**

The whole point of See the Other Side is aligning our hearts with the heart of Jesus.

Know/Grow/Go - love is the driving force.  
We are made to love the One who made and loves us.  
Out of love, God prepared a garden for humanity to thrive; out of love, Jesus gladly chose to bear the cross; out of love, He made the Kingdom of God available and present.  
The Gospel is the way we learn to be human; the Gospel teaches us how to love again.  
So our love for God - to know Him, grow in Him, and tell the world about Him - is not merely curiosity, but more like a hunger.  
There's no love without sacrifice. The grace we received freely from God is not cheap love, it cost the precious blood of Jesus. There's also a cost of discipleship. We need to bear our cross daily. But remember, his yoke is easy, his burden is light.

Salvation is only the beginning, there's so much grace on every step of sanctification.  
As part of the Know/Grow/Go model, evangelism is part of the discipleship - the way we walk with Jesus Christ, to carry out his vision and mission. Discipleship is a long obedience in the same direction.  
It's about who we are becoming.  
"To be human is to be for something, directed toward something, oriented toward something." - James K. A. Smith  
It's about living out the new creation.
