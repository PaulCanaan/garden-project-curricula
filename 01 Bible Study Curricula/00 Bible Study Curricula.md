# Bible Study Curricula

Scripture-centered formation journeys developed within [[00 Curricula Library|The Garden Project's Curricula Library]]. Each curriculum should help participants read the Bible as the unified story of God's work in Christ and inhabit that story through worship, community, practice, and mission.

## Curricula

- [[00 Planted|Planted]] — creation, fall, Christ's death and resurrection, kingdom perspective, formation, church, and mission through the image of a seed
- [[00 Covenant Kingdom and People of God|Covenant, Kingdom, and People of God]] — the biblical covenants, kingdom, presence, and the formation of God's people
- [[00 God With Us|God With Us]] — build the temple story through Blueprint, Construction, Reconstruction, Remodel, and Expansion, then inhabit God's presence through [[T2T]]
- [[00 Thus Says The LORD|Thus Says The LORD]] — learn to hear, walk with the prophets through new exodus, covenant, temple, King, and people, then [[Embody the Voice|embody the Voice]] as Christ's witnesses

## Curriculum standard

Every publishable Bible study should include:

1. A transformation promise and intended audience
2. A governing biblical-theological thesis
3. A coherent session arc and recurring learning rhythm
4. Careful exegesis in literary, historical, and canonical context
5. Explicit connections to Jesus Christ and the whole biblical story
6. Formation practices that move beyond information transfer
7. Facilitator guidance, pastoral boundaries, and accessibility alternatives
8. Participant materials that can stand apart from private research notes
9. Sources, permissions, versioning, pilot evidence, and revision history

## Garden Project formation commitments

- **Slow down:** make room for attention rather than rushing to answers.
- **Encounter God:** read Scripture as people addressed by the living God.
- **Learn from Jesus:** keep the person, teaching, cross, resurrection, and way of Jesus central.
- **Practice together:** join interpretation to prayer, table, community, obedience, and mission.
- **Cultivate fruit:** evaluate learning by growing love and faithfulness, not recall alone.
- **Form CoJourners:** invite mature participants to help others inhabit the story.
