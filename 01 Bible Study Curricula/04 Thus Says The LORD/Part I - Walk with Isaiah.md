# Part I — Walk with Isaiah

## God Delivers — The New Exodus

**Big question:** How will the Holy One rescue his people and renew creation?

## Chapter 1 — A Holy God and a Sinful People

- **World:** the Assyrian crisis and the reigns from Uzziah through Hezekiah
- **Texts:** Isaiah 1; 5–7; 30–31
- **Motifs:** holiness, rebellion, judgment, Zion, trust, hard hearts
- **Movement:** Judah's slavery is not only political; sin and failed trust create the need for a deeper exodus.

## Chapter 2 — The Servant Who Saves

- **World and design:** consolation to exiles; Isaiah 40–55 and the servant passages
- **Texts:** Isaiah 40; 42; 49; 50; 52:13–53:12
- **Motifs:** wilderness highway, shepherd, servant, redemption, substitution, mission to the nations
- **Canonical trajectory:** the Gospel opening, Jesus' mission, Luke 9's “exodus,” and Acts 8

## Chapter 3 — The New Exodus

- **Texts:** Isaiah 55; 60–62; 65–66
- **Motifs:** return, water, feast, nations, joy, new heavens and new earth
- **Movement:** deliverance grows beyond return from Babylon toward the renewal of creation.
- **Canonical horizon:** Revelation 21–22

## Formation movement

Confess false trusts, receive the Servant's deliverance, make straight paths of faithfulness, and practice hope that welcomes the nations.
