# Listening for the Echoes

Use after completing the contextual work in [[Prophetic World Timeline and Context Guide]]. Not every passage gives equal weight to every Echo.

| Echo | Listening question | Textual evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Upward** | What does this reveal about YHWH, worship, holiness, and covenant loyalty? | |
| **Backward** | What act, covenant, person, place, or promise in God's Story is recalled? | |
| **Inward** | What idol, sin, fear, desire, or heart condition is exposed? | |
| **Outward** | How should God's people treat one another and live before the nations? | |
| **Forward** | What judgment, deliverance, Messiah, Spirit, Kingdom, or creation hope is announced? | |

## From listening to formation

- What must we worship or refuse?
- What must we remember?
- Where must we repent?
- What communal practice or public witness is required?
- What promise gives us courage to endure?

## Christological check

Before claiming fulfillment, ask:

1. What did this text communicate in its prophetic setting?
2. What canonical pattern or promise develops from it?
3. How does the New Testament receive or develop it?
4. How does Christ fulfill rather than merely resemble it?
