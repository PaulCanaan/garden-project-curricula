# Thus Says the LORD Resource Reservoir

This reservoir feeds curriculum research, facilitator preparation, and a future tiered book-club appendix. Inclusion marks a conversation partner, not unqualified endorsement. Verify editions, arguments, and permissions before publication.

## Start here

- John H. Walton — *The Lost World of the Prophets: Old Testament Prophecy and Apocalyptic Literature in Ancient Context*
- J. Daniel Hays — *The Message of the Prophets*
- Gordon D. Fee and Douglas Stuart — *How to Read the Bible for All Its Worth*
- Richard L. Pratt Jr. — *He Gave Us Prophets* (free Third Millennium teaching series rather than a print book)
- Christopher J. H. Wright — *The Mission of God's People*

## Ancient Near Eastern and cultural context

- John H. Walton — *Ancient Near Eastern Thought and the Old Testament*

- K. Lawson Younger Jr. — *Ancient Conquest Accounts*
- K. A. Kitchen — *On the Reliability of the Old Testament*

Ancient context should illuminate the prophets' communicative world without becoming the controlling authority over the canonical text.

## Living Witness Library

Develop a later collection of biographies and primary texts showing faithful Christian witness in varied settings. Initial figures for research include Dietrich Bonhoeffer, John M. Perkins, Richard Wurmbrand, and Lesslie Newbigin. Choose specific works only after evaluating historical context, theological contribution, and suitability for the audience.

## Future book-club tiers

| Tier | Purpose | Likely authors/resources |
|---|---|---|
| Accessible | Learn how prophecy works and meet its message | Walton, Hays, Fee and Stuart, Peterson |
| Intermediate | Study major corpora and biblical-theological themes | Motyer, Oswalt, Block, House, Christopher Wright |
