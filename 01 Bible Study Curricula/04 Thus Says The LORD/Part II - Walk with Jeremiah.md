# Part II — Walk with Jeremiah

## God Renews — The New Covenant

**Big question:** How can covenant-breaking people remain God's people?

## Chapter 1 — The Broken Covenant

- **World:** Judah's final kings, Babylon's rise, and Jerusalem's approaching fall
- **Texts:** Jeremiah 2–3; 7; 11
- **Motifs:** marriage and adultery, idolatry, false temple confidence, covenant lawsuit, uncircumcised heart
- **Background:** Deuteronomy's covenant blessings, curses, and call to return

## Chapter 2 — A New Heart

- **Texts:** Jeremiah 30–31
- **Motifs:** return, forgiveness, Torah written within, knowing YHWH, restored relationship
- **Theological center:** renewal must reach deeper than external repair; God must remake the covenant people from within.

## Chapter 3 — Hope Beyond Exile

- **Texts:** Jeremiah 31–33, including the field purchased during siege
- **Motifs:** divine faithfulness, Davidic hope, restored community, future covenant life
- **Canonical trajectory:** Jesus' covenant meal in Luke 22 and Hebrews 8–10

## Formation movement

Name covenant-breaking without false security, receive forgiveness, practice an inwardly formed obedience, and enact hope before circumstances change.
