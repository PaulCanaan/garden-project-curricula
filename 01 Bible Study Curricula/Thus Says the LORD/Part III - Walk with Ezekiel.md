# Part III — Walk with Ezekiel

## God Dwells — The New Temple

**Big question:** How can the holy God dwell among his people again?

## Chapter 1 — When Glory Departs

- **World:** Judean exiles in Babylon and Jerusalem's collapse
- **Texts:** Ezekiel 1; 8–11
- **Forms:** throne vision, symbolic imagery, temple tour, enacted judgment
- **Motifs:** glory, mobility of divine presence, idolatry, holiness, judgment, exile

## Chapter 2 — A New Heart and a New Spirit

- **Texts:** Ezekiel 34; 36–37
- **Motifs:** true Shepherd, cleansing, new heart, Spirit, dry bones, reunited people
- **Movement:** restoration requires both revived people and the presence that makes them live.

## Chapter 3 — When Glory Returns

- **Texts:** Ezekiel 40–48, especially 43 and 47
- **Motifs:** temple, measured holiness, returning glory, river, fruitful land, God's abiding name
- **Canonical trajectory:** Jesus as temple and source of living water; Spirit-indwelt Church; Revelation's garden-city

## Formation movement

Reject domesticated views of God, receive the Spirit's re-creating work, become a holy dwelling, and carry life toward barren places.
