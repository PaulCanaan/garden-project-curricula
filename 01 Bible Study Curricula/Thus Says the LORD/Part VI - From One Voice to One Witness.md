# Part VI — Embody the Voice

## From the Prophets to Christ to the Church

**Goal:** Move from hearing diverse prophetic witnesses to recognizing their fulfillment in Christ and bearing faithful witness as his Church.

## Chapter 1 — One Voice

### Christ: The Word Made Flesh

- **One Story:** creation, covenant, Kingdom, exile, restoration, new creation
- **One Messiah:** prophet, priest, King, servant, shepherd, Son of Man, branch, bridegroom, temple
- **One Kingdom:** God's saving reign for Israel, the nations, and creation
- **Anchor texts:** Luke 24:25–27, 44–49; John 1:1–18; Hebrews 1:1–4

Christological reading follows the prophets' canonical trajectories; it does not erase what their words communicated within Israel's history.

## Chapter 2 — One Witness

### The Church Bears Witness to the Word

The Spirit joins proclamation and communal life so the Church becomes a truthful sign of Christ's Kingdom among the nations.

- Spirit-empowered witness — Acts 1–2
- Ambassadors of reconciliation — 2 Corinthians 5
- Royal priesthood proclaiming God's excellencies — 1 Peter 2
- Patient witness under beastly power — Revelation

The Church does not replace the prophets or produce a new canon. Under Christ and the apostolic witness, it receives, proclaims, and embodies God's revealed Word.

## Chapter 3 — Embody the Voice

Use [[Embody the Voice]] as five integrated ways of bearing witness:

1. **See** — discern God's reality amid false stories.
2. **Feel** — share God's holy compassion rather than merely being correct.
3. **Speak** — tell the truth with wisdom, love, courage, and fitting timing.
4. **Live** — make the Gospel visible through holiness, justice, hospitality, generosity, reconciliation, peacemaking, and faithful presence.
5. **Hope** — endure, pray, rejoice, and wait for God's promised future.

## Commissioning

We have learned to hear the Voice, walked with those who received it, and met the Word made flesh. Now we bear witness—not as original voices seeking attention, but as a people whose words and shared life faithfully echo Christ.
