# See the Other Side

## Purpose

Cultivate resurrection-shaped perception by helping participants see the empty tomb, the already-and-not-yet Kingdom, and eternity as God's life with humanity.

## Central question

How does resurrection change what we see and how we live now?

## Key texts

- Mark 1:14–15 — the Kingdom has drawn near
- Matthew 5–7 — the right-side-up life of God's reign
- John 17:3 — eternal life as knowing God through Jesus Christ
- 1 Corinthians 15:35–49 — resurrection through the image of a planted seed
- Ecclesiastes 3:11 — eternity placed in the human heart amid limited vision

## Program and frameworks

- **Program:** [[Learning to Handstand in 5 Minutes]]
- **Kingdom framework:** [[Already Not Yet]]
- **Formation framework:** [[Grace Truth Matrix]]

## Three perspective shifts

1. **See the other side of the empty tomb:** death is real but resurrection changes the ending.
2. **See the other side of the Kingdom:** the Beatitudes reveal God's reign as a reality that challenges ordinary measures of power, success, and blessing.
3. **See the other side of eternity:** eternal life is communion with God beginning now and fulfilled in new creation, not merely escape after death.

## Growth over time

The manuscript adds time as a third dimension to the Grace Truth Matrix:

> **Growth = (Grace + Truth) × Time**

This is a teaching metaphor, not a quantitative formula. It emphasizes that Christlike formation ordinarily requires sustained grace, truthful encounter, patience, and God's action across seasons.

## Storyline

There is no Kingdom apart from the cross. Resurrection inaugurates new creation and reorients growth toward God's promised future. Paul plants, Apollos waters, and God gives the growth (1 Corinthians 3:6–7).

## Formation response

- Identify one circumstance being interpreted only by present appearance.
- Ask what Christ's resurrection and promised future make visible without denying present pain.
- Choose one patient practice of grace and truth for the next season.
