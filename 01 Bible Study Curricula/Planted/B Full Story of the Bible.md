# Full Story of the Bible

## Governing movement

**Creation → Fall → Redemption → New Creation**

The Bible tells the true Story of God and God's people from creation to new creation, centered on Jesus Christ.

## Half-story distortion

A shortened gospel can begin only with human badness and end only with individual escape after death:

1. Humans are bad.
2. God is good.
3. Believers go to heaven; unbelievers go to hell.

Those claims touch real biblical concerns—sin, holiness, judgment, salvation, and life with God—but detached from creation and new creation they can flatten the gospel, obscure vocation, and treat salvation as escape from God's world.

## Full-story proclamation

1. **Creation:** God creates a good world and gives humanity communion, identity, and vocation.
2. **Fall:** sin fractures relationship with God, neighbor, self, and creation and brings guilt, bondage, exile, and death.
3. **Redemption:** Jesus lives faithfully, bears sin, dies, rises, reigns, and gives the Spirit, reconciling a people to God and inaugurating new creation.
4. **New Creation:** God will dwell with humanity, judge evil, raise the dead, heal the nations, and renew creation.

## Why the beginning and ending matter

- Creation tells us what salvation restores and fulfills.
- New creation tells us where redemption is going.
- Christ is not an emergency detour but the center in whom creation's purpose and promised future cohere.
- The Church is formed to inhabit and witness to this Story in the already and not yet.

## Use in Planted

Each movement of [[00 Planted]] retells this same Story through the seed:

- planted in good creation;
- buried amid sin and death;
- raised with Christ into new life;
- growing toward the Kingdom's promised future;
- becoming a fruitful people who plant again.
