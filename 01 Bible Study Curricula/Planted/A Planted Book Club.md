# Planted Book Club

The manuscript pairs each movement with two books. These are optional conversation partners, not participant prerequisites.

## Chapter pairings

### 1. There Is a Garden Named Delight

- James Bryan Smith, *The Good and Beautiful God*
- Carmen Joy Imes, *Being God's Image*

### 2. Devil Not Today

- John Mark Comer, *Live No Lies*
- Louie Giglio, *Don't Give the Enemy a Seat at Your Table*

### 3. Breakdown Is the Beginning of Breakthrough

- John Eldredge, *Beautiful Outlaw*
- N. T. Wright, *Surprised by Hope*

### 4. See the Other Side

- John Eldredge, *All Things New*
- John Ortberg, *Eternity Is Now in Session*

### 5. From Planted to Plant

- Dietrich Bonhoeffer, *Life Together*
- N. T. Wright, *After You Believe*

## Additional reading

- John Mark Comer, *Garden City*
- Nancy Guthrie, *Even Better than Eden*
- John Stott, *The Cross of Christ*
- George Eldon Ladd, *The Gospel of the Kingdom*
- Dallas Willard, *The Divine Conspiracy*
- Dallas Willard, *Renovation of the Heart*
- James K. A. Smith, *How to Inhabit Time*
- Banning Liebscher, *Rooted*
- Eugene Peterson, *A Long Obedience in the Same Direction*
- Matthew Sleeth, *Reforesting Faith*
