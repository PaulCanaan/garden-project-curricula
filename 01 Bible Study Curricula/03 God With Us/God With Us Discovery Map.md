# God With Us Discovery Map

This map preserves the active source document's architectural hypothesis while separating canonical movements from proposed correspondences.

| Movement | Dwelling stage | Divine presence | Priestly sphere | Mission field |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Blueprint** | Garden temple | Garden where God walks with humanity | Land of Eden cultivated and guarded | World beyond Eden |
| **Blueprint** | Tabernacle / proto-temple | Ark and mercy-seat symbolism in the Most Holy Place | Holy Place and priestly ministry | Courtyard, Israel's camp, and vocation among the nations |
| **Construction / Reconstruction** | Jerusalem temple | Most Holy Place | Holy Place, priesthood, and covenant life | Courts, Israel, resident foreigners, and nations |
| **Remodel** | Jesus | Fullness of God dwelling bodily | High Priest and sacrificial Lamb | Israel and the nations |
| **Remodel** | Church | Holy Spirit | Body of Christ, royal priesthood, living sacrifices | World to which the Church is sent |
| **Expansion** | New Jerusalem | God and the Lamb fill the city | Servants reign and worship before God | Nations walk by its light; creation is healed |

## How to use the map

For each dwelling stage:

1. Establish the historical and literary context.
2. Locate presence, priestly service, and outward vocation from the text.
3. Name similarities and differences from earlier stages.
4. Trace development through Christ rather than jumping directly to personal application.
5. Record what the proposed correspondence clarifies and what it leaves unexplained.

## Status and cautions

- The trifold structure is a developing interpretive tool, not a claim that every temple has three physically identical concentric zones.
- “Holy Place” and “inner court” are not interchangeable technical terms.
- The mission field sometimes names space, sometimes people, and sometimes vocation; do not hide that categorical shift.
- Edenic priesthood and cosmic-temple readings should be taught with textual grounding and awareness of scholarly debate.
- Jesus fulfills Israel's temple story uniquely; Church correspondences are participatory through union with Christ and the Spirit.

## Recurring discovery questions

- Where and how is God's presence revealed?
- Who serves, mediates blessing, guards holiness, or offers worship?
- Toward whom or where is life and blessing meant to extend?
- What has developed from the previous stage?
- What remains unfinished until Christ and new creation?
