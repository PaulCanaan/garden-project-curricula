## Stable design decisions

- The curriculum is level 300 and part II of the *Sojourners;* arc.
- Presence and homecoming are the governing theological and emotional centers.
- The core journey is Blueprint → Construction → Reconstruction → Remodel → Expansion.
- The trifold discovery lens is Divine Presence → Priestly Sphere → Mission Field.
- [[Building A House]] is the experiential entrance.
- [[T2T]] is the formational and missional exit.
