# Week 1 — Blueprint

## Cosmic Temple → Garden Temple → Tabernacle

**Governing question:** What pattern does God establish for rest, dwelling, priestly vocation, and mission?

## Draft word-study focus

- *shavat* — cease/rest; study Genesis 2:2–3 within creation's completion
- *mishkan* — dwelling/tabernacle; study Exodus 25:8–9 within covenant and sanctuary instructions

Use [[Bible Word Study Practice]] and verify forms, glosses, and semantic ranges before teaching.

## Key texts

- Genesis 1–3
- Exodus 25:8–9; 40:34–38
- Leviticus 26:11–12

## Discovery

- In Genesis 1–2, what signals suggest God is not only making but settling into creation?
- What similarities and differences appear between Eden and the tabernacle?
- How do divine presence, priestly service, and outward mission relate?

## Trifold investigation

| Dwelling | Divine presence | Priestly sphere | Mission field |
|---|---|---|---|
| Garden temple | Garden where God walks | Land of Eden cultivated and guarded | World beyond Eden to be filled and ordered |
| Tabernacle | Ark and mercy seat in the Most Holy Place | Priestly ministry associated with the Holy Place | Courtyard, camp, and vocation of Israel among the nations |

The table is provisional. Avoid treating land, rooms, courts, and peoples as exact equivalents.

## Teaching movement

Genesis 1 portrays God ordering the cosmic house; Genesis 2 focuses on the garden home where God dwells with humanity. Exile from Eden begins the problem the temple storyline addresses. The tabernacle becomes a mobile sanctuary among a pilgrim people, echoing creation while anticipating a fuller homecoming.

## Formation

- **Reflect:** Am I seeking rest merely as relief, or as covenant communion with God?
- **Practice:** Attend to God's presence in ordinary created space without confusing creation with the Creator.
- **Prayer:** Ask to become a faithful cultivator and guardian of the life entrusted to you.

## Development questions

- How should Genesis 1 as cosmic temple be presented as an argued interpretation rather than an uncontested conclusion?
- How should *avad* and *shamar* support the garden-priesthood connection?
- How should the distinction between the garden and the wider land of Eden be explained from Genesis 2:8?
