# Already Not Yet

## Core insight

In Jesus Christ, God's kingdom and new creation have truly begun, but their fullness awaits his return, resurrection, judgment, and the renewal of all things.

## Already

- Christ has defeated sin and death decisively.
- The Spirit gives new-covenant life.
- Believers participate in resurrection life, communion, and God's kingdom.
- The church becomes a sign and foretaste of new creation.

## Not yet

- Sin, suffering, injustice, and death remain present.
- Sanctification and mission continue through pilgrimage.
- Resurrection and cosmic restoration await consummation.
- Hope resists both despair and premature triumphalism.
