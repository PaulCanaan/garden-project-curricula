# Embody the Voice

## Bearing Witness to the Word

> **The prophets received God's Word. Jesus is the Word made flesh. The Church bears witness to the Word.**

Embody the Voice is the exit framework for [[00 Thus Says The LORD]]. It moves prophetic study toward a Christological and ecclesial rule of witness: the Church listens to God's revealed Word, is conformed to the incarnate Word, and makes the Gospel audible and visible in the world.

## Five ways the Church bears witness

| Movement | Vocation | Formation | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|---|
| **See** | Discern God's reality | Become discerning | What is true here, what idols or false stories obscure it, and where is God at work? |
| **Feel** | Share God's heart | Become compassionate | What does holy love grieve, confront, cherish, and seek to heal? |
| **Speak** | Proclaim God's truth | Become truthful and courageous | Is the witness true, loving, timely, responsible, and centered on Christ? |
| **Live** | Embody God's Kingdom | Become a living witness | Do our holiness, justice, hospitality, generosity, reconciliation, and common life agree with our words? |
| **Hope** | Await God's future | Become steadfast | How do God's promises enable prayer, endurance, joy, and faithful action? |

The order matters. Speech follows attentive discernment and compassion; it is tested by embodied integrity and sustained by hope.

## Christological center

Jesus perfectly embodies God's prophetic witness:

- he sees people and the Father's work;
- he is moved with compassion;
- he announces the Kingdom and speaks truth;
- he enacts the message through service, holiness, suffering, death, and resurrection;
- he endures for the joy set before him.

The framework therefore forms witnesses to Jesus, not people seeking a self-authorized prophetic identity.

## Church practices

- **See:** Scripture-shaped cultural discernment, attentive listening, communal examination
- **Feel:** lament, intercession, proximity to suffering, celebration of mercy
- **Speak:** Gospel testimony, truth in love, advocacy, confession, wise silence
- **Live:** worship, justice, hospitality, shared life, reconciliation, vocation, faithful presence
- **Hope:** prayer, Sabbath, perseverance, joy, patient resistance, anticipation of new creation

## Biblical grounding

- **Prophets receive:** Jeremiah 1:4–10; Ezekiel 2:8–3:3; Amos 3:7–8
- **Word made flesh:** John 1:1–18; Luke 4:16–21; Hebrews 1:1–4
- **Church bears witness:** Acts 1:8; 2 Corinthians 5:18–20; Philippians 2:14–16; 1 Peter 2:9–12; Revelation 12:11

## Guardrails

- Faithful witness is not contrarianism for its own sake.
- Compassion without truth becomes sentiment; truth without compassion becomes violence.
- Speaking must not eclipse listening, repentance, or the credibility of shared life.
- The Church's witness is derivative and accountable to Christ, Scripture, and apostolic teaching.
- “Embody” does not mean the Church replaces Christ; it participates by the Spirit in his mission.
