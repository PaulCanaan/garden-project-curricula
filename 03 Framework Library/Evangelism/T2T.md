# T2T

## From Tabernacle to Temple — From Temple to Tabernacling Presence

T2T is the exit framework for [[00 God With Us]]. The biblical movement travels from the mobile tabernacle toward temple and climactically toward Jesus, the Church, and new creation. The missional movement sends the Spirit-indwelt temple-people outward as a tabernacling presence during their earthly sojourn.

The acronym's final public expansion remains a design decision. Its current logic is the paired movement **Tabernacle → Temple** and **Temple → Tabernacling**.

## The two movements

### Biblical pattern — Tabernacle to Temple

- God dwells among a pilgrim people in a mobile sanctuary.
- Temple gathers presence, priesthood, worship, and Kingdom in a settled house.
- Jesus fulfills temple as the incarnate Son, High Priest, and sacrifice.
- The Spirit builds the Church together as God's dwelling.
- New creation expands sacred space until God and the Lamb fill the garden-city.

### Church mission — Temple to Tabernacling

The Church does not carry God as though he were absent elsewhere. The Spirit-indwelt people make God's reconciling presence visible through a shared life patterned by Christ.

## Three practices

### 1. Worship — Attend to God's presence

- **Temple logic:** sacred space exists for right response to God's nearness; sacrifice becomes embodied life in Romans 12:1.
- **Personal practice:** Scripture, prayer, silence, gratitude, and beginning the day by acknowledging God's presence.
- **Communal practice:** gatherings centered on God rather than performance, joining Scripture, confession, praise, table, and sending.
- **Teaching line:** Worship is not how we invite God in; it is how we live because he is already with us.

### 2. Witness — Make presence visible

- **Temple logic:** Israel is called to witness; Jesus is the faithful witness; the Spirit sends the Church.
- **Personal practice:** coherent life, natural testimony, truth joined to love.
- **Communal practice:** reconciliation, justice, mercy, creativity, and congregations as signs of the coming Kingdom.
- **Teaching line:** We do not bring God with us; we reveal the God who dwells in us.

### 3. Hospitality — Extend welcome

- **Temple logic:** God's dwelling gathers a people; Jesus welcomes sinners and outsiders; the Church becomes an open household in Christ.
- **Personal practice:** open tables, shared meals, attentive listening, and welcome without manipulation.
- **Communal practice:** spaces that are safe, relational, and hospitable to honest seeking.
- **Teaching line:** Hospitality is what happens when God's dwelling place becomes accessible.

## Biblical grounding

- **Worship:** Exodus 40; Psalm 27:4; Romans 12:1–2
- **Witness:** Isaiah 43:10; Acts 1:8; 1 Peter 2:9–12
- **Hospitality:** Luke 14; Romans 12:13; Hebrews 13:1–2
- **Tabernacling mission:** John 1:14; Ephesians 2:19–22; Revelation 21:1–3

## Guardrails

- God's omnipresence is not expanded by Church activity; mission reveals and participates in God's reconciling presence.
- Hospitality does not erase holiness, safeguarding, boundaries, or the call to transformation.
- “Belong before believe” is a pastoral posture of welcome, not a complete theology of membership or covenant identity.
- The Church participates in temple identity only in union with Christ and by the Spirit.
- Worship, witness, and hospitality overlap; they are practices, not three sealed departments.
